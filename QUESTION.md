# Main question

How can existing communities, green spaces and local organisations in Eindhoven form a mutually supportive ecosystem that contributes to human and ecological health through shared care?

## Context

This landscape grows out of the Build for Eindhoven event and a conversation with Maartje.

- Community is the foundation.
- Connection is the operating principle.
- Shared care and maintenance are essential for continuity.
- Human and ecological health are the intended outcomes.

Three strategic pillars remain leading:

1. Strengthen De Wielewaal as a central green motor.
2. Connect existing initiatives into a city-wide ecosystem.
3. Bring nature closer to people's everyday lives through accessible initiatives.

Starting points named in the commission: Voedselbos Eindhoven, De Wielewaal, Wasven, Bij Albrecht, Phood, Victoriapark and their wider networks.

## Working constraints

- Research with the community in mind, not on its behalf.
- Distinguish documented relationships from inferred connections and unexplored opportunities.
- Do not assume willingness to collaborate.
- Ecological limits and sensitive habitats must be protected.
- Timebox the work; reuse existing evidence; do not contact stakeholders; do not start implementation.

The candidate research questions live as GitHub issues in this repository.
