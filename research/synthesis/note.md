# What the seven November-2026 passes establish when read together

**Synthesis note, 26–27 November 2026.** This note retrieves nothing new. It reads the seven passes commissioned in the 26 November 2026 wave — [#4](../complementarity/note.md), [#5](../reciprocity/note.md), [#6](../limits/note.md), [#9](../community/note.md), [#10](../health-outcomes/note.md), [#12](../coordination-ai/note.md), [#14](../history/note.md) — against each other and against the merged notes they extend. A revision of [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) landed in the same pull request.

**Marks.** Where a pass marks a claim **[D]**, this note repeats it as *that pass's finding*, not as a claim retrieved here. Claims this note makes *about* the seven — two passes share one document, a figure drifts, x and y cannot both hold — are marked **[S]** and carry locators so a reviewer can attack them. **[A]** marks a finding of the independent cross-note audit; **[V]** a finding of the independent figure verification. Both were read-only and neither wrote to the repository.

**What it answers, and what it does not.** It answers: *read together, what do these seven establish that none establishes alone, and where do they disagree?* It is not a summary of the seven and not a substitute for them. Findings that appear in one pass only — #14's genealogy, #9's ten guardrails, #10's claim ladder — are underrepresented here by construction.

## 1. The answer in short

**1. One organisation carries the documented connective function — but the evidence for it is narrower than it looks, and this is the first thing the synthesis has to get right.** Five passes reach Trefpunt Groen: as the sole recipient of the city's green budget-subsidy line and the city's own "Netwerkorganisatie" (€232,000, [#4](../complementarity/note.md) §1.3) **[D]**; as the body spending 20% of paid hours on relational work under a *samenwerkingsprotocol* whose tree advice binds ([#5](../reciprocity/note.md) §3, §6) **[D]**; as the adviser whose ecological advice the college declined against ([#6](../limits/note.md) §1.4) **[D]**; as the host in which the one surviving Proeftuin040 strand, the Groen Café, continued ([#14](../history/note.md) §1.3) **[D]**; and as the two-coordinator body absorbing municipal advisory work ([#12](../coordination-ai/note.md) §1.1) **[D]**.

**But "five passes" is not five witnesses, and the audit says so in terms [A]:** the Groen Café series resolves to a **single document** — Trefpunt's *Jaarverslag 2025* — repeated in four notes (§3.1); the turnover figure is that same document again; and two of the seven reach Trefpunt only through relays of merged notes. **What survives is still substantial:** the *municipal register* names Trefpunt as the sole green recipient (one producer), and the *payee's own accounts* give a figure equal to it (a second, independent producer). Those two producers agree, and that agreement is the real finding. The claim that must **not** be made is the one this note first drafted: that Trefpunt's €232,000 is its whole income from the municipality. Nobody established the funder split; #4's own `sources.md` records it as **[U]** **[A]**.

**2. The binding constraint is secured position, decided in writing before an initiative applies.** Four passes reach this from different evidence: municipal award decisions (a 15-year lease published as a *voornemen tot uitgifte*, a single-candidate parcel justified by "fragmentatie van het beheer", a ten-year DAEB designation 2026–2035) ([#4](../complementarity/note.md) §1.1); programme evaluations and a lease notice in which the city says it is "onvoldoende geëquipeerd om grote aantallen vrijwilligers te bereiken en te begeleiden" ([#5](../reciprocity/note.md) §1.2); a 2013 erfpacht award twelve years before the closure ([#14](../history/note.md) §1.4); and the zoning, leash and APV instruments already governing the pillar-1 site ([#6](../limits/note.md) §1.2–1.3) **[S]**.

**One correction the audit forces [A]:** "no competitive green money at all" is too broad. The *Ruimtelijk Domein* budget-subsidy line does have one organisational recipient, but the same register carries an owner-only greening subsidy of up to €2,500 that four other notes document. The defensible form is narrow: **one organisational budget-subsidy recipient in that line of the 2025 publication.**

**3. Every durable arrangement has an identifiable payer, and volunteer energy is what is lost when the payer leaves.** #5's central finding, corroborated from different material: the one Proeftuin040 line that reproduced is the one inside a *stichting* with paid coordination and an institutional venue ([#14](../history/note.md) §1.3, §6.1); the deepest documented volunteer base (150 regular volunteers, 0 fte paid staff in that foundation) sits inside the most institutionally secured organisation ([#4](../complementarity/note.md) §1.7) **[S]** **[A: 1 producer, two editions — see §3.1]**. In Eindhoven the volunteer commons is what institutions are made of, not what competes with them.

**4. The numbers the ecosystem is most likely to quote measure something else.** Five passes each found one such figure, and the failure mode is the same — a unit, denominator or definition that does not survive the sentence **[S]**:
- The participation counts are **counts of municipal codings**: of 19 records rated "high", 13 entered via an area coordinator, and the rating has no published criterion ([#9](../community/note.md) §1.1) **[D]**. **[V]** recomputed from the export to the unit: 124 records, hoog 19 / redelijk 24 / matig 26 / laag 22 / uncoded 33; of the 19, geco 13. Denominator note: 19 is 21% of the 91 *coded* records, not of 124.
- "**+2 gezonde jaren**" for green is a per-indicator expected gain in a city-wide publication, attached to a budget performance indicator, with **no derivation, population, period or counterfactual** ([#10](../health-outcomes/note.md) §1.1) **[D]**. **[V]** confirmed the absence in two further municipal documents and found the *Bijlagebundel* recording that substantiation was given orally at a participation event and never published.
- There is **no carrying-capacity threshold** for any named site; the 300 m / 30% canopy figures are a national heuristic that says "Vuistregels zijn niet verplicht"; the 75 m² figure is a **housing** norm ([#6](../limits/note.md) §1.2, [#10](../health-outcomes/note.md) §1.2) **[S]**.
- The "**six-node network**" was a **rendering artefact and is withdrawn**; Trefpunt claims "ruim honderd groepen" **[C]** ([#4](../complementarity/note.md) §1.6) **[D]**.
- The giving-versus-asking gap (**9 in 10** against **4 in 10**) has unit *individual volunteer* and object "mensen" in general — **not** evidence that organisations hesitate to ask each other ([#5](../reciprocity/note.md) §1.4) **[D]**.

Rule that follows: **state the definition before the number, and publish the denominator with it.**

**5. Nobody in the record speaks for themselves — but the claim is narrower than the repository has been stating it, and one pass states it falsely.** [#19](../meaning/note.md) established participant silence for the closed food forest. [#9](../community/note.md) extends it to the green *data*: the register's 124 records contain no name, contact or verbatim field **[D]**. [#5](../reciprocity/note.md) reaches the same absence through programme evaluations **[D]**.

**The audit materially corrects the four-way convergence [A]:** it is **three independent searches of three different corpora plus one relay**, and:
- **The absolute form is false as written.** [#10](../health-outcomes/note.md) states that "no verbatim volunteer or neighbour account exists in the public record" — and it is a relay of [#19](../meaning/note.md), whose finding was scoped to the anchor case. [#5](../reciprocity/note.md) in fact **documents exceptions**: two volunteers quoted on a scheme's own webpage and a coordinator quoted by an evaluator; [#9](../community/note.md) has one quoted volunteer line and one resident statement.
- [#9](../community/note.md) also frames itself as *confirming* the existing finding, so one of the four is an echo by construction.

The honest statement is therefore: **no participant voice appears in the municipal registers, the programme evaluations or the health instruments searched — the genres that would carry one — and where a quote does appear it is a single line mediated by the producer.** This is still a strong negative about the public record. It is not proof that nobody speaks, and no pass searched outside these genres.

**6. Software is not the missing layer, but the evidence is thinner than the claim.** Of ten documented coordination tasks, four justify *borrowing* a stateless tool and six justify doing nothing; none justifies building ([#12](../coordination-ai/note.md) §1.5) **[D]**. Its decisive input — the hours breakdown — is a single document (§3.1), and its own §2.4 concedes fast decay. Quote it as *"no case for building was found"*, not *"software is not needed"* **[S]**.

**7. Health has no institutional carrier on either side.** Green is a *justification* quantified in unpublished-derivation healthy years and a *performance indicator* in the budget cycle ([#10](../health-outcomes/note.md) §1.1); the regional health architecture lists 193 partners and not one garden or food forest — only Wasven ([#10](../health-outcomes/note.md) §1.1; #27 merged) **[D, 1 producer, 1 independent retrieval, 2 relays — A]**. So "human and ecological health" is an outcome in neither architecture. That is the sharpest strategic finding in the set.

## 3. How independent are the seven? A corroboration audit

The value of reading seven passes together rests entirely on this section. Seven reports written on one day by one programme, from the same municipal sources, will *look* convergent whether or not they are.

### 3.1 One document, many notes — the cases the audit found **[A]**

| Claim | Notes that state it | Underlying producers | Independent witnesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eight Groen Cafés in 2025 with Bibliotheek/KNNV/IVN/Wasven | #4, #5, #12, #14 | all resolve to Trefpunt's *Jaarverslag 2025* (or a relay of the merged network note) | **1** — the clearest case of a set that looks like four witnesses |
| Trefpunt turnover €232,000 | #4, #5, #12 | the same *Jaarverslag 2025* bytes, three times | **1**, repeated |
| €232,000 is Trefpunt's municipal line | #4 | the Woo subsidies register | **1** — but this one *agrees with the payee's own accounts*, a genuine second producer for the amount |
| Wasven 150 volunteers / 0 fte | #4, #5 | the same producer's ANBI form in **two editions** (FY2025 baten €414,097; FY2024 €310,993) | **1** — repeated publication, not corroboration; and neither note names the edition |
| 19 rated high / 13 via coordinator | #9 | one dataset, one computation | **1** — do not present as corroborated |
| "Structure is necessary and insufficient"; Boekel 1/5 | #12 (twice), brief | one file; and its source is the community's **own self-scoring** resilience tracker | **1, and a self-report** |
| 145 ha for De Wielewaal | #4, #6, #14 | no hectares figure is recorded in any of the three source registers | **not determinable**; at most 1, on three statements of one relay |
| 193 DE STAP partners; no garden in the list | #4 (own retrieval), #5 (relay), #10 (relay) | DE STAP list | **1 producer, 1 independent retrieval, 2 relays** |

### 3.2 What is genuinely independent

- **Secured position.** #4 reads award decisions and a subsidy register; #5 reads programme evaluations and a lease notice; #14 reads a 2014–2017 archive. Different documents, different institutions, compatible conclusions **[S]**.
- **The payer.** #5's *Groen Verbindt* evaluation (a national programme with no Eindhoven project among its participants), #14's local archive and #4's ANBI filing are three different producers reaching "a legal person with paid coordination" **[S]**.
- **The participation counts.** Recomputed from the raw export to the unit by an independent verifier **[V]** — the strongest single data point in the seven.
- **17 *buurt* strings vs nine *groenarme buurten*.** Three separate retrievals of one published dataset: **one witness for the fact, three for the arithmetic** **[A]** — solid arithmetic, but the underlying classification is still the city's own.

### 3.3 The participant-silence convergence, priced honestly

**Three independent searches of three different corpora (municipal registers; programme evaluations; health and ecological instruments) plus one relay, and the absolute form is falsified by the exceptions #5 itself documents.** Convergence across genres is real and is the reason the finding is worth keeping; calling it "four independent passes" would be false, and #9's self-description as confirming an existing finding makes one of the four an echo by construction **[A]**.

### 3.4 What this section cannot clear

Whether two passes citing different source ids cite different *documents* cannot be settled from the notes — it requires opening both `sources.md` files, which the audit did for the nine claims above and this note did not do for the rest. **This is a sample, not a clearance certificate.** The audit's own bottom line: everything else that looks multi-witness in this set is one document repeated, one dataset recounted, or a sibling-note relay passing as retrieval.

## 4. Where the passes contradict, narrow or correct each other

### 4.1 Four direct contradictions **[A]**

1. **The food forest's absence from Trefpunt's *achterban*.** #4 re-read the evidence and weakened it to **[U]** — the map node list is a rendering and the annual report carries no member list, so neither establishes absence. #5, #9 and #12 still assert it as **[D]**, on relays. **Use [U].** This matters because the negative was doing real work in the brief (§5).
2. **Whether any participant voice exists.** §1.5 — #10's absolute sentence is false as written and is a relay of a site-scoped finding.
3. **The register's planned-year count: 14 or 20.** #9 recounted 20 (2025: 11, 2023: 6, 2024: 3) from the whole export with a recorded hash and says the earlier figure was arithmetic drift; #12 still carries 14. **Use 20.**
4. **De Wielewaal's volunteer count: 120, ~100, or [U].** Unresolved **[A]**. #6 over-reads its own source — the register says "120 **geïnteresseerden**", interested people, not signed-up volunteers; #12 gives ~100 with no locator; #4 marks it **[U]**, which is the only defensible mark.

### 4.2 The house disagreement: administrative burden

The merged participation research says participation has a price "and it is mostly administrative", naming *regeldruk* ([`../participation/note.md`](../participation/note.md) §4) **[D]**. #12 says administration is 5% of a green body's paid hours, tied for the smallest of seven ([#12](../coordination-ai/note.md) §1.1) **[D]**. **Both are true of different bearers** — volunteers versus paid staff — and neither note said so **[S]**. A tool that removes the *volunteer's* burden is not addressed by the 5%, and the 5% does not license "administration is not the problem".

### 4.3 Where one pass holds half a truth

- **The route to green ground.** #4 says there is no route for a volunteer commons; #14 finds a documented route for "groenwaarde basis" but notes it is for the *least valuable* green; #4 separately records that sale is refused where the request is "om de grond als tuin in gebruik te nemen". **The joint statement neither note carries: a route exists for the least valuable green, and it is expressly closed to garden use** **[A]**.
- **De Vergeten Tuin.** #5 files it as an informality case; #9 and #14 document it as a members' association with a written 2025 management agreement — which #5's own §9 calls the one documented formalised quid pro quo with the city. #5's own evidence narrows its P10 **[A]**.
- **Wasven's financials** come from two different editions and the notes do not name which **[A]**. Quote an edition or do not quote the figure.

### 4.4 Smaller drifts that will silently spread

The 2024 market's participant count is **13 other organisations** upstream and is restated as 13 or 14 depending on whether the food forest counts itself **[A]**; the 0.7 ha is commissioner-provided **[P]** at origin and relayed as **[D]** in four notes; "no legal entity" is **[U]** at origin and **[D]** in a relay. None of these changes a conclusion. All of them are the mechanism by which the repository's marks inflate over time.

## 6. What this means for the main question

The main question asks how existing communities, green spaces and local organisations can form a **mutually supportive ecosystem**. Read together, the seven answer a prior question: *the field already has a shape — a funded advisory body plus hosted nodes — and the shape is documented, thinly.*

1. **"Form an ecosystem" is the wrong verb.** A connective function exists, is paid for, and is advisory over a membership whose extent is claimed but unaudited (**[C]** ~100 groups; no list). The live choice is *through*, *beside* or *instead* **[S]**.
2. **Working *through* has a documented cost.** 20% of that body's paid hours go on relational work and its tree advice binds **[D]**. Entry means accepting an advisory relationship that already represents an *achterban* — whether the closed food forest belonged to it is unknown (§4.1), which makes this a question to ask, not a barrier to assume.
3. **Working *beside* has a documented failure mode.** Proeftuin040 was resourced, mandated, ran four years and left no successor; the one strand that survived did so inside a body with a legal person and a venue **[D]**. A new connective layer without a host is the case the city has already run.
4. **Health carries none of this yet.** Green treats health as a budget indicator with an unpublished derivation; the health architecture lists no garden or food forest among 193 partners **[D]**. "Human and ecological health" is a justification in one and a performance indicator in the other, and an outcome in neither. For Voedselbos 2.0's visibility focus this is the sharpest consequence: **becoming visible to a field that already has a funded voice, in which visibility is not the binding constraint, will not by itself change what the field can hold** **[S]**.

**On pillar 1.** Nothing in the wave supports "De Wielewaal as central green motor". The estate is documented as a protected area with an all-day, year-round leash duty, a municipal management model and a users' council, counted by nobody in public **[D]** ([#6](../limits/note.md) §13). Keep it as the hypothesis the focus note calls it.

## 8. Limits of this note

- **It is a reading, not a retrieval.** Its evidence is the seven notes, plus two read-only verification passes. Where a pass is wrong, this note inherits the error unless §3–§4 caught it.
- **All seven were written on one day by one programme**, sharing a house style, mark scheme and commissioner. Convergence across them is weaker evidence than convergence across seven independent researchers, and §3 prices that rather than denying it.
- **It selects a story.** "Secured position and a payer" is one reading; a reader could foreground the volunteer-survival evidence or the ecological-instrument evidence instead. The selection is stated so it can be contested.
- **The audit found more defects than could be fixed here.** Nine relayed claims, four contradictions and four drifts are recorded in §3–§4; the corrections applied in place this wave are #4's lease status and sector compression and #6's quotation (§4 of the respective notes).
