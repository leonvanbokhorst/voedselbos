# The new / renewed TAC garden — verification

**Question:** what exactly is the new or renewed TAC garden? Desk check, 26 November 2026, public sources only; no stakeholder contact. Labels: **[D]** documented, **[C]** claimed (stated intention, not evidenced as built or agreed), **[U]** not established. Sources in `tac-garden-sources.md`.

**Headline: there is no documented "new TAC garden" as a built thing — no size, plan, design, opening date, manager or access rule appears in any source found. What exists is a retained corner garden (committed 2022), an intended courtyard garden (claimed 2022), and a hedged 2025 statement that "our lively garden of old is coming back", possibly with a herb garden. Anything more specific is not established.**

## 1. What is actually documented

**[D] TAC is mid-redevelopment, step by step.** Municipal news (02-12-2025): first ateliers handed over, 80 ateliers/studios, horeca, large exhibition space, 4,800 m², plus "TAC Living" (3 towers, 249 affordable rental homes, delivery planned November 2026) — in a district becoming "een levendige mix van wonen, werken, cultuur en groen". TAC's own site: first two halls open, more step by step; restaurant managed in-house, opens officially October 2026; national season opening 28–30 August 2026; **full opening weekend April 2027** **[D]**.

**[D] The 2025 annual report expects full operation "in de loop vanaf 2027"** and does not mention a garden at all — no tuin, pluktuin, greenhouse or green space appears in the bestuursverslag or the notes **[D, absence]**. The stichting owns TAC Horeca-exploitatie BV and TAC Vastgoed BV; result 2025 was −€85,934 **[D]**.

**[D] A corner garden exists and is retained.** In the 2022 co-creation report TAC commits: "De tuin op de hoek van de Ventoselaan en Gagelstraat wordt behouden, met vijgenboom en het oude hekwerk" **[D]**. This is a specific, located, retained garden — the only garden element in the record with a place attached to it.

**[D] A courtyard garden was promised in principle.** Same report, TAC's response: "De binnentuin van TAC zien wij graag voor ons als groene oase voor kunstenaars, bezoekers, directe omwonenden en buren in de wijk. Dat vergt een gezamenlijk plan. Dit zullen we in de komende maanden verder uitwerken" **[D]**. A green facade is planned on the Gagelstraat **[D]**. No later document found reports that "gezamenlijk plan" being made **[U]**.

## 2. What is only claimed

**[C] "Onze levendige tuin van toen komt weer terug! Wellicht met een kruidentuin en heel misschien zelfs een kas voor performances en bandjes."** This is TAC's own renovation page, hedged twice ("wellicht", "heel misschien") **[C]**. It is the closest thing to a statement about a *new* garden, and it states no location, size, designer, date, budget, manager or access rule.

**[C] The pluktuin is a resident suggestion, not a TAC commitment.** In the April 2022 session, neighbours asked for "een pluktuin voor horeca uit de buurt", height differences instead of flat lawns, nut trees and play elements **[D as a record of what residents said]**. TAC's written response endorses the *idea of a green oasis* and commits to the corner garden and a green facade — it does not adopt the pluktuin or the nut trees **[D]**.

**[C] Edible planting is not established anywhere**: no source describes existing edible planting at TAC, a harvest, a garden volunteer group or a food programme tied to a garden. The restaurant is managed in-house with "een leuke kaart" **[C]**; nothing connects its menu to on-site growing **[U]**.

## 3. Public, tenants-only, programmed or community garden?

**[C] Semi-public by intention, undefined in practice.** The only stated intention is a courtyard as "groene oase" for artists, visitors, "directe omwonenden en buren in de wijk" **[C]**. That is a user description, not an access rule.

**[U]** Opening hours, whether the courtyard is reachable without a ticket, whether the corner garden is fenced (residents asked to keep "het hekwerk rond de tuin"), who maintains it (TAC's building management? a gardener? volunteers?), whether tenancy of an atelier includes garden use, and whether any community-garden governance (plots, membership, rules) exists. None of this is in the record.

**Management is TAC's.** TAC is the stichting that owns the property companies and now runs its own hospitality **[D]**. No municipal or partner role in a garden was found — the municipality's involvement documented is planning of the surrounding streets ("Het ontwerp van de straten rond TAC wordt nog door de gemeente ingevuld", 2022) **[D]**, not the garden itself.

## 4. Relation to the redevelopment, restaurant and food-design programme

- **Redevelopment:** the garden was one of four zones in the 2022 co-creation (street/entrance, garden, programme, buildings) **[D]**, but it is absent from every later status document read — municipal news, TAC's renovation page beyond the one line, the annual report **[D, absence]**. In a delayed project now targeting April 2027, the garden is not on the published critical path.
- **Restaurant:** TAC manages it itself, opening October 2026 **[D]**. A restaurant plus a herb garden is the obvious pairing — TAC's hedged line points that way — but **no source states the kitchen will use garden produce** **[U]**.
- **Food design:** none found. TAC's programme identity is art, studios, exhibitions, DDW/GLOW and artist development (Inversie) **[D]**. No food-design strand appears in the 2025 report, and none of the food-design actors in this research has a documented TAC link **[U]**.

## 5. What this means for the research

The TAC garden is a **plausible but unverified green node**: a retained corner garden with a fig tree, an intended courtyard "green oasis", a hedged herb-garden ambition, and a neighbourhood pluktuin wish nobody adopted. It is not evidence of edible planting or community use and should not be cited as one. The document that would settle it — a definitive design or the April 2027 opening programme — does not exist publicly yet. It is also a different green element from the Victoriapark in the same district.

## 6. Not established

Size, location and design of any new garden; whether the courtyard garden was built; opening date; access rules; who maintains it; whether edible or pluktuin planting exists or is planned; whether the kitchen uses it; any community-garden governance or partner; whether the 2022 "gezamenlijk plan" was ever made; the budget. The annual report's silence is the strongest available signal that the garden is not yet a defined project.
