## Access notes and boundaries

- The TAC annual report (T3) was read as extracted text (76,785 characters) and searched for garden terms; the absence of any garden mention is a finding about that document, not proof that no garden exists.
- `raadsinformatie.eindhoven.nl` disallows crawling in `robots.txt`; T7 and T8 were visible only as search-result snippets and were deliberately not opened.
- No local press article specifically about a TAC garden was found; searches returned the sources above instead. That absence is reported rather than worked around.
- TAC's social accounts were not opened. No page under tac.nu describing a garden, pluktuin, or garden programme other than T1 was found.
- Nothing here implies any current plan, willingness or commitment by TAC, the municipality or any neighbour beyond what is quoted.
