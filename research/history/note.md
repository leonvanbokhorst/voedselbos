# How did this network come to be — and what do past collaborations teach?

**Issue:** [#14](https://github.com/leonvanbokhorst/voedselbos/issues/14).
**Lens declared:** **founding myth versus record** — where an initiative's own origin story and the retrievable documents diverge, and whose account became the official one.
**Research date:** 26 November 2026. Public record only. No interviews, no contact, no forms, no logins. Desk research.
**Marks:** **[D]** documented in a retrieved source (source id in brackets); **[C]** claimed by one actor or a self-description; **[I]** inference of this note; **[U]** not established in the sources searched; **[P]** provided by the commissioner (direct account, not public documentation). ## 1. The answer in short

1. **The city has already run this experiment once, and the food forest was inside it.** Proeftuin040 launched publicly on **30 January 2014** as *Platform Stadslandbouw Eindhoven*, in the Van Abbemuseum, in a minicongres organised by **Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven** and opened by wethouder Mary-Ann Schreurs **[D, S1]**. Its stated reason for existing is the one in the issue: to answer initiatives that "mostly operated individually" **[D, S4]**. It was handed a council mandate in December 2014 to write an urban-agriculture vision *with* the field, delivered that vision in July 2016 **[D, S12]**, kept working as a project vehicle through 2017, and then **stopped — with no closure notice, no dissolution, no successor organisation and no published account** **[D, S6/S9/S10]**. What a new ecosystem attempt in 2026 faces is therefore not virgin ground but a **vacated position**: a connective layer was tried, resourced, and lost, and almost nobody wrote down why.

2. **The food forest was not outside the network. It was inside the 2014–2016 one.** A 2015 Proeftuin040 participant list names "Lise Alix: Sociologe, **voedselbos** en Rechtstreex" and "Arjan de Vries: Resiliant Culture" **[D, S4]**; both appear in the *GROW — Natural Edible Urban* collective in October 2014, which ran a mini-conference *with* Proeftuin040 **[D, S16]**; the food forest's founders are recorded elsewhere as "Lize and Arjan" **[D, S21]**. **That these are the same two people is an inference, not established** **[I]**, and one strand of the identification is weakened (§5.3). If it holds, the correct statement of the problem changes: the food forest's founders were *in the room* for the city's last connective attempt, and both that room and that food forest are now gone.

3. **What survives is what was hosted by an institution with a reason to keep it.** The one Proeftuin040 activity with a documented continuous afterlife is the **Groen Café** — "een initiatief van Proeftuin040, de Bibliotheek Eindhoven en Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven" in 2017 **[D, S17]** — which by 2025 runs as Trefpunt Groen programming with the Bibliotheek, KNNV and IVN, eight sessions that year **[D, X3]**. The pattern in the second case (§6.1) is not "good idea": it is **a legal person with paid coordination plus an institutional venue**.

4. **The municipal instruments select for the opposite of a volunteer commons, and they did so before 2014.** In **February 2013** the city granted Philips Fruittuin a municipal building on **20-year erfpacht** on the Fruittuin's own ground, **€650,000 fully covered by the province** under *Samen Investeren Brabantstad*, in exchange for a 20-year operating and education duty **[D, S20]** — with **Trefpunt Groen** named among the programme's partners **[D, S20]**. That is a community-green instrument in shape and a capitalised-partner instrument in effect, and it is twelve years older than the food forest's closure. Nothing comparable exists for a group on land it does not own **[D/I, X4]**.

5. **Five things "worked", and every one of them worked through a host, a named person or a written arrangement — not through network energy.** Documented: a facilitated co-design that produced a garden still in use four years later (Tineltuin, 2015→2019) **[D, S5/S32/S33]**; a themed series that survived its originator inside a stichting (Groen Café) **[D, S17/X3]**; a project with named institutional partners that produced a physical, visitable asset (BeeWare040's hive route) **[D, S5/S6/S18]**; a hybrid forum that put a municipal process in a room with the field — two ~35-person collective sessions a month apart, then a city debate at the town hall **[D, S4]**; and a zoning-plus-agreement route to secure ground (De Vergeten Tuin, 2007 + 2025) **[D, X2]**. None of these required a legal entity for the *initiative*; all of them required one somewhere in the chain.

6. **The founding mythology is not a lie; it is a division of labour nobody wrote down.** The platform described itself as the field organising itself **[D, S11/S4]**; the consultancy that helped write the vision recorded that it was produced "**in opdracht van gemeente Eindhoven**" **[D, S12]**; the council minute records that the meeting which started it came from a **wethouder's budget-debate commitment** **[D, S2]**; the faction that tabled the motion records that the agenda was set by its **2013 initiatiefvoorstel** **[D, S13]**. Four true statements, four different authors, and the version that survived in the academic literature is the one that centres the citizens **[D, S4]**.

## 4. Proeftuin040: the reconstruction

### 4.1 What it was

A **knowledge platform**, not a programme, not a subsidy scheme, and — so far as any source shows — **not a legal person** **[D/I]**. Its own name for itself, in its website title from 2015 to 2018, was "Proeftuin040 – Platform voor stadslandbouw Eindhoven" **[D, S7/S8]**. TRANSMANGO calls it "the UA knowledge platform Proeftuin040" and "the organisation Proeftuin040" **[D, S4]**; a 2018 third party calls BeeWare040 "een dochter van Proeftuin040" **[D, S18]**. **No KVK number, stichting, vereniging, ANBI registration, annual report or board was found in any source searched** **[U]** — and its own project pages route contact through personal mailboxes (`andre@proeftuin040.nl`) **[D, S6]**.

This matters more than it looks. Everything the platform did was done by named individuals: **André Cools, Niek van de Klundert, Neelke Goossens** in the founding and in the day-to-day of the site and the sessions **[D, S1/S4/S5/S6]**, with one intern in 2017 **[D, S6]**. When the individuals stopped, nothing had to be wound up, because nothing had a legal body to wind up.

**What it said it did** (its own December 2014 slide, in its own words) **[D, S3]**: physical meetings ("samenbrengen initiatiefnemers", "behoefte/problemen polsen", "werksessies", "inloop/informatie avonden"); a website with a **shared agenda**, an inventory of initiatives, and "presentatie Eindhoven stadslandbouwstad!"; regional and national alignment ("Stedennetwerk Stadslandbouw", "Provinciaal overleg Stadslandbouw"); "Agenda Stadslandbouw — Stadslandbouw officieel op de gemeentelijke agenda krijgen"; "**Procedures vereenvoudigen** — in samenwerking met de gemeente, kortere eenvoudige procedures, aanmelden via website, **Energie van vrijwilligers behouden!**"; and the *Dag van de Stadslandbouw*.

### 4.2 Who ran it, on whose money

Named drivers: **André Cools** and **Niek van de Klundert** (aftrap, 30-01-2014) **[D, S1]**, with **Neelke Goossens** appearing as author of a large share of the 2015–2017 posts **[D, S5/S6]**. Affiliation: none found beyond "Proeftuin040". Support: a paid design/consultancy input — **Aarde & Co (Wieteke Brocken)**, a commercial studio with a "(Stads)Landbouw" line, who state they supported the vision "in opdracht van gemeente Eindhoven" **[D, S11/S12]**. Academic input: **Wageningen University (Rural Sociology / TRANSMANGO)** ran foresight workshops inside the platform's own process and supplied an expert meeting **[D, S4]**. Project partners: Food4Bees and IVN; **SNV Brabant Centraal**; ProRail; the Bijenkorf; Pullman; Van Abbemuseum; the Bibliotheek; Trefpunt Groen; TransitieStad Eindhoven; Groen Gennep; Woonbedrijf and WIJ Eindhoven in the workshop room **[D, S5/S6/S4]**.

**Money: no budget, grant, subsidy or municipal payment to Proeftuin040 itself was found in any source searched [U].** What the record does show is money *around* it: a municipal commission for the vision (amount unstated) **[D, S12]**; an EU research project that attached its workshops to the platform's process **[D, S4]**; and commercial partners who paid for hives, honey and workshops in kind or in contract **[D, S6]**. The platform's own listed ambition included "Procedures vereenvoudigen" and its own intern's 2017 assignment was an **MKBA of its own added value** **[D, S6]** — a platform that has to prove its own value has no secure line.

### 4.3 What it actually did, and what came of it

Three things left a trace after the platform.

**(a) The vision.** The 2 December 2014 stadsgesprek produced "draagvlak … voor het opstellen van een visie door het 'veld' zelf (van onderop)" **[D, S2]**; a December 2014 motion carried with broad support set the council to the task, "assigned to the earlier established Proeftuin040" **[D, S4/S5]**; the drafting ran through 2015–2016 with ~35 participants in each of two collective werksessies, a Stamtafel of about ten regulars, a city debate at the town hall in May 2016, and a scenario workshop in June **[D, S4]**; the document was delivered **in July 2016** **[D, S12]**. TRANSMANGO's own conclusion at that exact moment: "**There is little clarity as to what will happen after the UA vision for the city of Eindhoven is finalized this summer (2016). A translation of this document into active and coherent food policy to the city is still to be seen.**" **[D, S4]**

**Did it ever become policy? Not established.** No council decision, college decision, beleidsregel, kadernota or later instrument citing a *Visie Stadslandbouw* was found; the 2026 instruments the sibling notes document (Beleidsregel groen en water 2026; Verordening inwonersparticipatie; groensubsidie) address a different question and do not reference it **[U, X4]**. The vision itself is described by its own authors as available only "**op aanvraag**" **[D, S12]** — a document that has to be requested has not entered a public process. This is the single largest gap in the reconstruction, and it is the gap that decides whether the platform's three years produced an instrument or a PDF.

**(b) The hive route.** BeeWare040 — kicked off May 2015 with Food4Bees and IVN **[D, S5]** — is the platform's most visible output: hives on the Bijenkorf roof (June 2015), a design competition judged by the city ecologist, a first honey run sold through a delicatessen, then hives at Pullman (21-03-2017), the Van Abbemuseum (22-04-2017) and ProRail land (26-04-2017) **[D, S5/S6]**. It was still being described as running in spring 2018 **[D, S18]**. Its own fragility is documented on its own site: the Garden Mania site "**moest het veld ruimen, voor nieuwbouw op StrijpS**" **[D, S6]** — development displacing a green project, in one sentence.

**(c) The Bennekel garden.** Two documented design sessions with Bennekel residents in February and March 2015, in which "de bewoners, die elkaar voor die tijd geen van allen kenden" chose between variants Proeftuin040 had drawn **[D, S5]**. A garden existed by mid-2015 **[D, S32]**, survived a fire that destroyed its shed and hedge on 31 December 2015 **[D, S32]**, and is documented in use again on 18 July 2019 **[D, S33]**. **This is the closest thing in the record to a durable place produced by the connective layer** — four years and change, against the food forest's twelve.

### 4.4 Why and when it stopped

**When, as far as the record shows:** the blog's newest post at the 30 June 2018 capture is **2 November 2017** **[D, S6]**; the site still answered 200 in **November 2018** **[D, S10]**; a third party still called it an extant organisation with a "dochter" in **spring 2018** **[D, S18]**; the domain served redirects from 2021 and a resale page by **August 2025** **[D, S9/S10]**. The honest statement is: **activity thinned through 2017 and stopped; there is no dated end.**

**Why — what the record supports, and what it does not:**

- **The mandate ran out.** The platform existed to get urban agriculture "officieel op de gemeentelijke agenda" and to produce a vision **[D, S3/S4]**. Both were, on the record, achieved by mid-2016 **[D, S12]**. After that the platform's own posts are projects, excursions and news, not process **[D, S6]**. **Supported [D/I].**
- **Two people, no legal person, no line of money.** Three named individuals, one intern, personal mailboxes, no entity and no found subsidy **[D/U]**. The platform's own 2014 slide had already named the risk: "**Tijdelijk en vrijwillig versus duurzame stedelijke ontwikkeling**" **[D, S3]**. **Supported [D/I].**
- **Its political sponsorship moved on.** The councillor who prepared the December 2014 motion, **Rik Thijs**, is reported as the wethouder who met food forest residents in **June 2026** **[D for 2014, S13; C for 2026, X1]** — the same office, a different dossier. What the record shows about the council's attention to urban agriculture after 2016 is nothing **[U]**.
- **The vision was commissioned, and the commission ended.** A paid process has a terminus; an institution has a budget. **[I]**, grounded in S12.
- **Explicitly not supported: that it failed.** No source describes a failure, a conflict, a refusal or a collapse **[U]**. No source describes a success either. The record contains neither an obituary nor a eulogy, which is why the platform is invisible in 2026 municipal material while its successor activity is.
- **Also not supported: that a successor body took over.** The only successor is a *programme line inside a partner*: the Groen Café, documented as "een initiatief van Proeftuin040, de Bibliotheek Eindhoven en Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven" in 2017 **[D, S17]** and as Trefpunt Groen programming in 2025 **[D, X3]**.

### 5.2 What the genealogy shows

**The organisations did not reproduce. The people did.** Of the ~36 people in the room at Proeftuin040's first collective werksessie (16-11-2015) **[D, S4]**, the record shows at least two — **Arjan de Vries** and **Willem Doreleijers/Dorelijers** (the Toekomstmakers prize winner for a mobile chicken tractor, 17-11-2015 **[D, S5]**) — reappearing years later in **Voedselboskabouters** **[D, S23]**, and Arjan de Vries in **Beginhoven** **[D, S22]**. What did not reproduce is the room. A network whose units are individuals has a high mortality rate per unit and a near-zero institutional memory: nothing carries the 2015 discussion into 2026 except a WUR report nobody in the city cites **[D/I]**.

**The one line that did reproduce is the one that had a stichting in it.** Groen Café: three initiators in 2017, one of them (Proeftuin040) gone by 2018, and the series running eight times a year by 2025 with two of the original three plus KNNV and IVN **[D, S17/X3]**. The mechanism is not mysterious: Trefpunt Groen has a board, a protocol mandate and paid coordinators **[D, X3]**; the Bibliotheek has a building and a programming calendar.

**A third line reproduced under a different name and outside the green sector.** Supportpunt, the municipal-backed support desk, built the food forest's website in 2023 and remains available **[D, X1/X3]** — the connective infrastructure the city *did* institutionalise, and the one the food forest used for a website rather than for tenure or succession **[D/I, X1]**.

### 5.3 The food forest's place in this — and the limit of the inference

The chain runs: Proeftuin040's 16-11-2015 participant list names "**Lise Alix: Sociologe, voedselbos en Rechtstreex**" and "**Arjan de Vries: Resiliant Culture**" **[D, S4]**; both are named in the **GROW** collective of October 2014, whose programme includes "Mini-conference met Proeftuin040" **[D, S16]**; the food forest's founders are recorded as "**Lize and Arjan**" **[D, S21]**; and "Lise Alix" is independently documented as a Wageningen Rural-Sociology-trained researcher whose 2012 MSc thesis was on an allotment park, examined "excellent" **[D, S24]**.

**Why I mark the identification [I] and not [D]:** no retrieved source names the food forest's founders in full; the spelling differs (Lize / Lise); "Arjan de Vries" is a common name; and — the strongest counter-consideration — **Arjan de Vries's own published biography lists his food projects ("Eetbaar Plantsoen", "Stadshoeve de Tuin", "Tuintopia") and does not mention an Elburglaan food forest** **[D, S22]**. Bios go stale, but this one names projects on either side of the 2013–2015 window, which weakens rather than strengthens the match for *that* half of the pair.

**What survives the doubt:** the descriptor "**voedselbos**" attached to a named person in the platform's own participant list, in November 2015, is a **documented edge between the food forest's world and the last connective layer** even if the identity is wrong in one name **[D]**. It does not change the answer; it changes the emphasis. The food forest was not a stranger to the 2014–2016 network.

## 6. Five lessons, with evidence status

### 6.1 A connective layer survives on a host with a continuity interest and a legal person — not on a mandate and two people

**Evidence.** Groen Café: joint initiative 2017 → Trefpunt Groen programming 2025, eight sessions **[D, S17/X3]**. Proeftuin040: three named individuals, personal mailboxes, no entity, no found funding, silence after November 2017 **[D, S6/S10/U]**.
**Status:** established for the Groen Café case **[D]**; the mechanism is **inference [I]** from two cases with opposite outcomes and different ages.
**Condition under which it worked:** the host already had a board, a mandate and paid coordination, and the series was **themed and repeatable** rather than a one-off process.
**Falsifier:** a volunteer-only connective layer in this city that outlived its founders without acquiring a legal person.

### 6.2 A commissioned process produces a document; it does not produce an institution

**Evidence.** The vision was commissioned by the municipality and delivered in July 2016 **[D, S12]**; TRANSMANGO said in the same months that "there is little clarity as to what will happen after the UA vision … is finalized" and that "the capacity of involved actors to develop a coherent and transparent strategy on how to mobilize support, to create new alliances and partnerships, and to strengthen institutional backup is still rather embryonic" **[D, S4]**; the platform's activity after delivery is projects, not process **[D, S6]**; no adoption of the vision was found **[U]**.
**Status:** the **sequence** is documented **[D]**; that the commission *caused* the subsequent thinning is **[I]** and cannot be separated from the two-person, no-entity condition in §6.1.
**Condition under which it worked:** it did produce a shared language and a network — participants reported that "many of the participants were not aware of what was going on elsewhere related to urban agriculture in Eindhoven" and that "one of the main outcomes of this session was a better connected network" **[D, S4]**. A process can produce connection. It does not produce a body.
**Falsifier:** a commissioned vision in this city that acquired an institutional owner.

### 6.3 Facilitated co-design with the people who will use the place produces places, and places outlive platforms

**Evidence.** Tineltuin: two documented design sessions with residents who "each other did not know before", February–March 2015 **[D, S5]**; garden existing by mid-2015 **[D, S32]**; still in use 18 July 2019 **[D, S33]** — four years after the platform's last post. Contrast the *Inloopavonden* and the werksessies: hundreds of participant-hours, no place.
**Status:** **documented [D]** that the place exists and persisted; **inference [I]** that the co-design method is the reason (the alternative is that the residents would have built it anyway — I could not find a source to test this).
**Condition:** a defined physical site, a bounded number of sessions, a choice put to future users rather than a plan presented to them, and — critically — someone else's ground. (Tenure of the Tineltuin's site is **[U]** in the sources read.)
**Counter-evidence, from the same garden:** the shed and hedge fire of 31 December 2015 with neighbours reporting "jongens stonden hard te joelen" **[D, S32]** — a neighbourhood garden is exposed to the neighbourhood. The practices note records a parallel case of a street garden partly removed after complaints **[D, X2]**.

### 6.4 Project money keeps an organisation busy and does not build a network

**Evidence.** Proeftuin040's busiest documented years, 2015–2017, are a project portfolio: BeeWare040 (Food4Bees, IVN, ProRail, Bijenkorf, Pullman, Van Abbemuseum, Garden Mania), Bietje Buurten (SNV Brabant Centraal), excursions (Philips GrowWise, WUR), a prize competition **[D, S5/S6]**. Its *network* instrument — a shared agenda, an inventory of the city's initiatives, a stamtafel — is the part that is **unreadable in the archive**, not the part that is documented **[D/U, S7/S8/S10]**.
**Status:** activity documented **[D]**; the claim that project income crowded out network-building is **[I]** and *not* established — the record is equally consistent with the platform doing both and only the projects leaving a trace.
**What TRANSMANGO recorded instead:** "the movement relies heavily on adhoc temporary funding, personal commitment and volunteering" **[D, S4]** — a description of the field, written by outsiders, in 2016, that reads in 2026 as a description of the field.

### 6.5 The city's route to secure ground runs through a capitalised partner — and this predates the food forest by a decade

**Evidence.** 5 February 2013: a municipal building on 20-year erfpacht on Philips Fruittuin's ground, €650,000 fully covered by the province, the Fruittuin carrying a 20-year operating and education duty, Trefpunt Groen among the programme partners **[D, S20]**. 2025–2026: Elburglaan 12 leased to Natuurmonumenten because "De Gemeente is onvoldoende geëquipeerd om grote aantallen vrijwilligers te bereiken en te begeleiden"; Wasven's ground given in use on continuity grounds **[D, X1]**. Against: 0.7 ha on private land, no lease, no entity, closed 28 July 2026 **[D, X1]**. And the 2007 zoning + 2025 management agreement route that secured De Vergeten Tuin without ownership **[D, X2]**.
**Status:** the instruments are **documented [D]**; the generalisation that *no* route exists for an unincorporated group is **not established [U]** — the Beleidsregel groen en water 2026 does contain a route for "groenwaarde basis", and its Article 10 refusal applies to "middel of hoog" **[D, X4]**. The accurate claim is narrower and sharper: **the available route is for the least valuable green, and the interesting green is reserved for professional managers.**
**Falsifier:** a documented case of an unincorporated residents' group obtaining a lease, erfpacht or management agreement on municipal green of "middel" or "hoog" value since 2026.

## 7. Path dependencies most likely to constrain a new attempt

**7.1 Who owns the land, and who therefore has an instrument.** Municipal money follows municipal ground: €9.8 m Lichtbos and €600 k a year on 145 ha the city owns, beside a closed twelve-year food forest on private land **[D, X1/X4]**. The pre-2014 template (S20) shows how far this goes back: the city's community-green instrument of 2013 was a building on a private foundation's land, capitalised by the province, operated by the foundation. **A new ecosystem that does not solve tenure is a programme, not an infrastructure** **[I]**.

**7.2 The mandate sits with two bodies that were both in the room in 2013–2015.** Trefpunt Groen appears as a partner in the municipality's Stadspoort programme in **2013** **[D, S20]**, hosted Proeftuin040's launch in **2014** **[D, S1]**, sits on its participant lists in **2015** **[D, S4]**, and holds the surviving series in **2017–2025** **[D, S17/X3]**. Natuurmonumenten holds the Elburglaan 12 lease and the Wielewaal operation **[D, X1]**. Any new connective layer is therefore either a **duplicate** of Trefpunt Groen's convening function or requires **its** consent — and its mandate is advisory, representing an *achterban* to which the food forest never belonged **[D, X3]**.

**7.3 The 2014 ask was never converted into an instrument, so it is still the 2014 ask.** The platform's own agenda item was "Stadslandbouw officieel op de gemeentelijke agenda krijgen" **[D, S3]**; a motion carried in December 2014 **[D, S5/S13]**; a vision was delivered in July 2016 **[D, S12]**; no instrument followed that I could find **[U]**. In 2026 the nearest instruments are the *Beleidsregel* (Article 10 gate), the *groensubsidie* (owner-only, €2,500 cap) and the *uitdaagrecht* **[D, X4]**. **The "policy ask" of 2026 is recognisably the policy ask of 2014, which is evidence that the 2014–2016 process did not settle it** **[I]**.

**7.4 Who was left out of which process.** Documented exclusions, each with a source:
- **The volunteer base itself.** The rooms were organisations: municipality (Openbare Ruimte, NDE, MVO), Woonbedrijf, WIJ Eindhoven, GGzE, Futuris, IVN, Trefpunt Groen, TU/e, HAS, Design Academy, Brabants Landschap-adjacent bodies **[D, S4]**. TRANSMANGO nonetheless observed that "participants on the workshops were mostly civil actors with relatively few institutional representatives" **[D, S4]** — the two readings conflict; the list is institutional, the researchers' impression was not. Both are reported and neither is resolved.
- **High-tech industry and SMEs.** "the urban food movement in Eindhoven has not engaged with high-tech firms, small and medium businesses and/or larger enterprises in the region. It is also largely disconnected to rural movements" **[D, S4]** — while Philips was in the room for the vision and hosted an excursion **[D, S6]**. Note the tension: the *company* engaged; the *sector* did not.
- **Residents as a constituency.** Residents appear as design participants for one garden (Bennekel, 2015) **[D, S5]** — not as a body with standing in the vision process.
- **Newcomers and status holders**, the subject of Bietje Buurten, appear in the record as project participants, summarised by the project ("op gezette tijden zowel de vrijwilligers als de statushouders bevraagd … De conclusies daarvan hebben we uiteindelijk gebundeld") **[D, S6]** — never in their own words.
- **The city's two other food-forest-adjacent nodes** — the Henri Dunantpark residents' forest and the Wielewaal — appear nowhere in the platform's record **[U]**; the platform's geography was the centre, Strijp-S, the Groene Corridor and a handful of care institutions **[D, S4/S5/S6]**.

**7.5 Which funding lines exist, and which do not.** Exist: municipal capital on municipal land; provincial capital via *Samen Investeren Brabantstad* into municipal projects **[D, S20]**; a €2,500 owner-only greening subsidy **[D, X4]**; a €1.42 m VSB-funded volunteer programme proposing a flexpool and an umbrella foundation **[D, X4]**; project and philanthropic money for individual initiatives (BPD → Phood, ASML → Wielewaal) **[D, X3]**; EU research money that attaches to a process for its own duration (TRANSMANGO) **[D, S4]**. Do not exist, so far as any source searched shows: **a standing line for coordination of a green network**; a line for a group on third-party land; a line for the *connective* work itself as distinct from its projects **[U, X4]**. The 2017 intern's assignment — an MKBA of the platform's own added value **[D, S6]** — is what an organisation does when it is trying to build a case for a line that does not exist.

## 8. Whose history is missing

The record is written by municipalities, funders, universities and professionalised organisations. Sorted by who is absent:

1. **The volunteers.** The food forest's ~30 are recorded as a group, never as people **[D, X1]**. The Tineltuin's builders are "de buurtbewoners" and one unnamed neighbour quoted by a broadcaster **[D, S32]**.
2. **The residents who did the design work.** The Bennekel sessions are described from the facilitator's side; the residents' preferences, disagreements and reasons are absent **[D, S5]**. Whether the garden they chose is the garden they got is **[U]**.
3. **The people who did not join.** No source in this search records anyone declining, leaving, or being excluded from any of these processes **[U]** — which is a property of the record, not evidence of harmony.
4. **The platform's own end.** There is no account of Proeftuin040's last year, no farewell, no handover note, no evaluation beyond an intern's assignment **[D/U, S6]**. Twelve years later the food forest got a closure notice; the platform got nothing.
5. **Migrant participants.** Photographed by a named photographer and summarised by the project; not quoted **[D, S6]**.
6. **Anyone outside the professional green circuit.** The high-tech and rural sectors, and the city's eastern and northern green, are documented as absent from the movement by the movement's own researchers **[D, S4]**.
7. **The losing options.** The 2016 vision's own manifest has eight key points (lifelong learning; residents feeling responsible; a smaller foodprint; a socially inclusive and healthy city; a green attractive city; city and countryside connected; urban agriculture professionalised; Eindhoven a frontrunner) **[D, S4]**. Which of them were argued for and dropped, and by whom, is **[U]**.

**And one structural absence worth naming for the lens:** the *official* account of Eindhoven's urban agriculture in 2014–2016 is, in practice, **a Wageningen University report in English** **[D, S4]**. The municipality's own record holds one minute, one slide deck and one invitation **[D, S2/S3/S1]**. The platform's own voice survives only as a dead Wix site, an RSS feed and a domain-resale page **[D, S6/S9]**. The account that became official is the one written by the outsiders who were invited in — because they were the only ones who wrote anything at all.

## 9. Counter-evidence and tensions

**9.1 A history of collaboration, or a history of competition?** The retrieved record is overwhelmingly a record of *collaboration*: Proeftuin040 was launched by Trefpunt Groen **[D, S1]**; its sessions were co-hosted with TransitieStad Eindhoven and Groen Gennep **[D, S5]**; BeeWare040 was a joint project with Food4Bees and IVN **[D, S5]**; Bietje Buurten with SNV **[D, S6]**; the Groen Café with the Bibliotheek and Trefpunt Groen **[D, S17]**; the 2024 market with thirteen organisations **[D, X3]**. **Not one documented rupture, split or refusal was found in any source searched** **[U]**. That absence is itself the strongest argument that this network's problem is not conflict but **dissipation** — and it should be read with suspicion, because promotional and institutional records do not record friction. The nearest thing to a documented conflict in the material is a **neighbour** removing a street garden after complaints and a **fire** at a neighbourhood garden **[D, X2/S32]** — friction at the scale of the street, not of the network.

**9.2 Institutionalisation as survival, or as loss of community character?** Both are documented, on different objects. **Survival:** Struikroven went from bewonersinitiatief to "een gewoonte binnen het Duurzaamheidspact" to a national foundation working on commission; Buurttuin vol Kleur runs as a youth-work programme with paid supervisors; the Groen Café survives inside Trefpunt Groen **[D, X2/S17/X3]**. **Loss:** each of those moves relocates the activity from residents to a commissioner, and in none of them is a participant's voice on the record **[D/I, X2]**. Proeftuin040 is the third case: it had no institution to be absorbed into, so it lost the lot — except the one line a partner took. **A new ecosystem should expect to be asked which of these three it is offering** **[I]**.

**9.3 Growth or stagnation?** The count of initiatives moves in the record without a method: **38** "currently active in Eindhoven" in the WUR report of mid-2016, sourced to Proeftuin040's own website **[D, S4]**; "**tegen de 50**" four months later, from the platform's spokesperson **[D, S19]**. Both are the same actor's estimate. Neither is a census. Twelve years on, the repository's own notes cannot enumerate the population either — six extractable nodes from Trefpunt Groen's map, no published total **[D, X3]**. **Growth in the count and growth in the network are different claims, and neither is established** **[U]**.

**9.4 Founding myth versus record — the four divergences.**
- **Who convened the turning-point meeting.** The platform's own post: "het door **GroenLinks en Proeftuin040 georganiseerde** stadsgesprek op 2 december 2014" **[D, S5]**. The council's minute: "Aanleiding tot de bijeenkomst is de **toezegging van wethouder Schreurs bij de begrotingsbehandeling op 28 oktober 2014**" **[D, S2]**. GroenLinks' own paper: the 2013 initiatiefvoorstel "heeft stadslandbouw op de agenda van de gemeenteraad gezet. Met dit stadsgesprek **gaf de raad** hier gevolg aan" **[D, S13]**. Three true descriptions, three different authors, and the one that centres the platform is the platform's.
- **Who wrote the vision.** "Van onderop", "door het veld zelf" **[D, S2]**; "made and formulated by citizens of Eindhoven" **[D, S4]**; D66's aspiration, "die visie hoofdzakelijk bedacht moet worden door degenen die ermee bezig zijn" **[D, S13]**; and then, in the consultant's small print, "**in opdracht van gemeente Eindhoven** ontwikkeld" **[D, S12]**. The commissioned status appears in exactly one place, and it is the place least likely to be read.
- **When it started.** "Founded early 2014" **[D, S4]**; the invitation for the "**officiële aftrap**" of 30 January 2014 **[D, S1]**; "de eerste Dag van de Stadslandbouw **sinds onze officiële oprichting als platform**", 17 May 2014 **[D, S5]**. These are compatible — but the platform's own public founding was *inside another organisation's event*, which none of the three accounts mentions.
- **When the hives started.** Proeftuin040's own post: **23 June 2015** **[D, S5]**. A 2018 third-party retelling: "**in juni 2017** werden er twee kasten en een korf … geplaatst" **[D, S18]**. Two years' drift in five, in a friendly retelling of a documented event.

**9.5 The tension the platform itself named, in 2014, and never resolved.** Its own December 2014 slide closes on three lines: "Actief burgerschap in een land vol regels en wetten"; "De lokale overheid: faciliteren en stimuleren of faciliteren en afwachten"; "**Tijdelijk en vrijwillig versus duurzame stedelijke ontwikkeling**" **[D, S3]**. Read in 2026 against the closure of a twelve-year volunteer food forest at the edge of a €9.8 m municipal estate **[D, X1]**, that slide is the most accurate document in this file. The people in the room in 2014 had already diagnosed the problem. What they did not have was an instrument.

## 13. Implications for the three pillars

**Pillar 1 — De Wielewaal as central green motor.** The historical record supports the sibling notes' caution and adds a precedent. The **Philips Fruittuin** settlement of February 2013 **[D, S20]** is what "an estate as a motor" actually looks like in this city's own instruments: a municipal building on a private foundation's land, twenty-year terms, provincial capital, an operating duty in exchange, and a named partner list that includes Trefpunt Groen. It is a **contract**, and it required a counterparty that could sign it. A Wielewaal that motors a network would need an equivalent agreement with a community counterparty. No such counterparty exists in the record **[U]**.

**Pillar 2 — a city-wide ecosystem.** The corrected statement is not "there is no connective layer". It is: **a connective layer was built in 2014, held a mandate, ran ~10 multi-stakeholder sessions in 18 months, delivered a vision in July 2016, and dissolved between 2017 and 2019 with no record of why** **[D, S1–S12]**. Any new attempt inherits three things from it: (a) a **vacated position** — no organisation now does what Proeftuin040 did, but Trefpunt Groen does the neighbouring thing; (b) an **unadopted policy product** — the 2016 vision is presumably still in a drawer, and asking the municipality for it is a free first move; (c) **a proven failure mode** — unincorporated, underfunded, two people, no succession — which is *exactly the same failure mode the food forest had*, and the same one the continuity note identifies **[D, X1/I]**. That symmetry is the most useful single finding in this note: **the connective layer and the node it was supposed to connect died of the same disease, five years apart.**

**Pillar 3 — nature closer to everyday life.** The cheapest documented successes are local and bounded: a co-designed neighbourhood garden that outlived its facilitator by four years **[D, S33]**, and a themed café series inside a library **[D, S17/X3]**. Neither needed a vision, an entity or a network. A pillar-3 programme that wants durable results should be looking at **who hosts, who is named, and what written arrangement exists** — not at how many initiatives can be listed.

**On Voedselbos 2.0.** The historical evidence gives 2.0 one clear warning and one clear opportunity. The warning: **visibility was never the binding constraint in this city's history** — Proeftuin040 had a Van Abbemuseum programme, a museum honey line, a Bijenkorf campaign, a national network affiliation it claimed for itself, and press coverage, and it still evaporated **[D, S6/S14; S3 for the network claim]**; what it did not have was an entity and a successor. The opportunity: the 2014–2016 network's **people are still traceable** (Voedselboskabouters, Beginhoven, Trefpunt Groen, the Bibliotheek, Woonbedrijf, WIJ Eindhoven) **[D, S22/S23/S17/X3]**, and the city's own record still holds the participant list **[D, S4]**. A successor that wants a network does not have to invent one; it has to be introduced to one that already existed and was never written down.

## 14. Limits of this note

- **No delegation was possible.** Two bounded subagent briefs (a dated founding genealogy, and a municipal/provincial policy-and-land timeline) were prepared and refused by the session's child-agent limit, which was already saturated by sibling researchers. **Both lines were therefore done by me alone, to a lower standard than intended**, and the date coverage is uneven: the pre-2014 and 2017–2022 municipal policy chain is carried largely from the sibling notes rather than retrieved fresh here. This is a real degradation and it is stated rather than repaired.
- **One witness where two would be better.** The July 2016 delivery of the vision rests on a single consultancy page **[D, S12]**. The 2015 Groen Café joint-initiative framing rests on one mailing **[D, S17]**. The food forest's founding date rests on "ten years ago" in a 2023 text **[D, S21]**.
- **The archive is thinner than the activity.** The platform's own inventory of the city's initiatives — the single document that would most change this note — is archived and unreadable **[U]**. The absence of a population count is a retrieval failure as much as a record failure.
- **Absence of a record of failure is not absence of failure.** No rupture, split or refusal was found. Institutional and promotional records do not log friction; a 2017 intern's MKBA, which might have, is not public.
- **This note is history of organisations, not of people.** By the commission's exclusion there are no interviews. Everything here about what the work meant to anyone is a description of a document, and the largest gaps in §8 are gaps that only interviews close.
- **No source read here supersedes the sibling notes.** Where this note and a sibling disagree — for instance on whether the food forest had any connection to the city's wider green network, which §5.3 now qualifies — the tension is stated in both places rather than resolved unilaterally.
