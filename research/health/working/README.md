**Marks.** The repository uses **[D]** documented in a retrieved source, **[C]** claimed by one actor or self-description, **[I]** inference, **[U]** not established, **[P]** commissioner-provided. All four delegated files use these; each states its own read depth per source (full / partial / abstract / snippet) and keeps its own access log.

**Delegation record.** Four bounded researchers on disjoint files, each told to retrieve rather than recall, to state read depth, to hunt unflattering evidence, to return "not established" as a result, not to contact anyone, not to bypass any access control, and not to spawn further agents. They did not read each other's files. The Lead re-read twelve documents at source before relying on them; what was re-read, what changed, and what was **not** re-verified are listed in [`../note.md`](../note.md) §14. One duplication was found and counted once: two lines retrieved the same UK green social prescribing evaluation — one witness, not two.

**Revision record (v2).** The revision was **not** delegated: one reviser, no subagents, on the same retrieval day. It added the three files above and re-read nine sources at greater depth than v1 had (listed in [`../revision-record.md`](../revision-record.md) §3). Two witness-count warnings were added: the SDT gardening study is **nested in the JArDinS cohort** (one research programme, not two witnesses), and the Dutch Platform31 article and its Flemish republication are **one source**. What v2 did **not** re-read — the Positieve Gezondheid critiques, the municipal monitor figures, the GGzE vacancy, the Fontys annual-report and BRON passages — remains single-retrieval exactly as v1 recorded it.
