# Line B — Regional money and institutional architecture, Eindhoven–De Kempen 2025–2026

**Parent question (context only):** To what extent is the health-promotion and prevention landscape in Eindhoven and Eindhoven–De Kempen fragmented, in what consequential ways, and what explains it; and under what conditions can autonomous initiatives contribute to shared outcomes without surrendering local identity.

**This line's question:** What does the documented institutional and financial architecture for health promotion look like in 2025–2026 — who is made responsible for what, how the money is structured, and what evidence exists of actual awards and implementation rather than plans and intentions?

## 1. Memo

**One scheme, three payees, and a ceiling raised twice to fit them.** The region's prevention money does not flow through a competitive grant market. It flows through a single municipal regulation — the *Subsidieregeling Preventie Infrastructuur IZA Eindhoven – De Kempen* — held by the municipality of Eindhoven, which acts as mandated penvoerder for the fifteen municipalities of the samenwerkingsregio [D]. The scheme's eligible applicant is not "any initiative" but a *penvoerder*: "de rechtspersoon die de subsidieaanvraag namens het samenwerkingsverband indient", where the samenwerkingsverband must be one "voortkomend uit het Regioplan" [D]. The municipal application page is blunter: "Aanvragen is alleen mogelijk voor IZA-partners, die **nu al penvoerder zijn** in de samenwerking voor het Regioplan" [D, municipal gloss]. Entry to the money is therefore through pre-existing Regioplan structures, not through open competition.

**The rulebook is permissive on money and thin on co-financing.** There is no co-financing or own-contribution requirement anywhere in the fourteen articles; eligible costs are uitvoeringskosten (including the cost of preparing transformatieplannen for insurer/ZonMW applications) and personele kosten [D]. Distribution is first-come-first-served, with loting for same-day arrivals — a queue, not a quality contest [D]. Refusal grounds are defensive rather than selective: a *voorliggende voorziening*, an excluded prioritaire opgave, or activities already funded by another SPUK in the same region [D]. The scheme is time-boxed: activities must run 1 January 2024 – 31 December 2026, and the regeling lapses 1 January 2027 [D].

**Awards are concentrated and, in one case, already unwound.** In spring 2025 exactly **three** applications were received, totalling €2.5 million: GGD Brabant-Zuidoost ("Programma Gezond opgroeien en leven voor iedereen"), Stroomz Zorgt ("De STAP naar Samen Precies") and Coöperatie Slimmer Leven ("Vitale communities en bewustwording") [D]. Because the total exceeded the €2.2m ceiling, the ceiling was raised to €2.5m — explicitly and only to cover those three, with the nota stating that "geen andere partijen een beroep kunnen doen op de ophoging" [D]. The same operation was repeated for 2026 [D]. This is the opposite of rationing: demand set the ceiling, and the ceiling was moved to demand. There was no competition to lose.

**Implementation shows the architecture's fragility.** Coöperatie Slimmer Leven — one of the three payees, holding €1.2 million — was **dissolved on 1 January 2026**; GGD took over penvoerderschap for 2026 as a one-year bridge, with partners required to sign a *garantstelling* [D]. Elsewhere the region diagnosed itself: the 2026 herijking records "veelheid aan activiteiten belegd in verschillende thema's (fragmentatie)", "verschillende samenwerkingsverbanden en governance structuren" and work "zonder werkelijke focus op de uitkomsten" [D]. A parallel €5.4m transformatiegeld stream required a fresh mandaat because the existing one was "juridisch niet toereikend" [D].

**What this establishes for the parent.** Responsibility is vertically split by design: GGD+STROOMZ set regional kaders for the five ketenaanpakken, municipalities implement locally [D]. Money is horizontally concentrated in three incumbents and vertically channelled through one mandated municipality. Fragmentation here is not primarily *many small grant-seekers competing*; it is *few penvoerders, parallel money streams, and a governance layer that the region itself judges to be under-integrated*. Rules alone establish none of this — the awards and the dissolution do.

## 4. Verbatim excerpts

**4.1 Penvoerder and the closed door [D]**
> "Voor subsidie komt uitsluitend in aanmerking een penvoerder, zijnde een rechtspersoon, namens een samenwerkingsverband voortkomend uit het Regioplan die zich verbonden en verenigd heeft rondom een prioritaire opgaven op één van de thema's of de regionale preventie infrastructuur zoals bedoeld in artikel"
> — CVDR721954 art. 3 (S1). *Gloss: only a penvoerder, a legal person acting for a Regioplan-derived partnership, qualifies.* Note the article **ends mid-sentence** ("zoals bedoeld in artikel" — no cross-reference) in all three versions; a drafting defect [D].

> "Aanvragen is alleen mogelijk voor IZA-partners, die nu al penvoerder zijn in de samenwerking voor het Regioplan en zich verbonden en verenigd hebben rondom één van de genoemde onderdelen in het Regioplan"
> — gemeente Eindhoven subsidy page (S5). *Gloss: only IZA partners who are already penvoerder may apply.* This is **stricter than the regulation's text** and is the clearest documented entry barrier [D for the claim; the regulation itself only requires a Regioplan-derived verband].

> "Samenwerkingsverband : … Het staat te allen tijde open voor het aansluiten van nieuwe partijen."
> — CVDR721954 art. 1 (S1). *Gloss: the partnership is at all times open to new parties joining.* New parties may join, but penvoerderschap is effectively incumbent-held.

**4.2 Distribution is a queue, not a contest [D]**
> "Verstrekking van subsidie vindt plaats op volgorde van ontvangst van complete aanvragen, totdat het voor deze regeling vastgestelde subsidieplafond is bereikt."
> "Indien het vastgestelde subsidieplafond dreigt te worden overschreden … worden de aanvragen die op die dag ontvangen zijn, door middel van loting gerangschikt."
> — CVDR721954 art. 9(2) and 9(4) (S1).

> "Het subsidieplafond voor 2024 bedraagt € 2.070.000, voor 2025 € 2.500.000,- en voor 2026 € 2.500.000."
> — CVDR721954 art. 9(1), v3 (S1). Earlier: "voor 2024 bedraagt € 2.070.000 en voor de jaren 2025 en 2026 € 2.200.000" (v1, S2); "voor 2025 € 2.500.000,- en voor 2026 € 2.200.000" (v2, S3).

> 3. Coöperatie Slimmer Leven: Vitale communities en bewustwording"

> "De ophoging is direct gekoppeld aan de ingediende aanvragen, waarop nog niet is beschikt. Dit betekent dat er geen andere partijen een beroep kunnen doen op de ophoging van het plafond en hiermee is dan ook geen sprake van benadeling van andere partijen."
> — Adviesnota 28-04-2025 (S6). *Gloss: the raise is tied directly to the submitted applications; no other party can draw on it.*

## 7. Consequential observations for the parent question (flagged as inference)

- **[I]** The architecture produces *concentration without competition*: a first-come-first-served scheme whose ceiling was raised twice to match exactly the demand of three incumbents. Scarcity was not used to select; the ceiling moved. Any claim that the scheme "excluded" applicants by rationing money would be unsupported by these sources.
- **[I]** The real exclusion mechanism, if any, is at the **door**, not the queue: penvoerderschap is limited to legal persons already leading a Regioplan-derived verband, and the municipal page states this as "nu al penvoerder". An autonomous local initiative cannot be a penvoerder on its own terms; it must be inside a Regioplan verband, represented by someone else's legal person.
- **[I]** Fragmentation in this region is **vertical and procedural** more than horizontal and competitive: regional kader-setting (GGD+STROOMZ) versus local implementation (15 municipalities); SPUK-IZA versus SPUK-Transformatie middelen with a *second, separately defective* mandate; subsidies versus opdrachten versus ambtelijke inzet inside one municipal budget. The region's own 2026 herijking says as much.
- **[I]** The dissolution of Coöperatie Slimmer Leven mid-subsidy — with a one-year GGD bridge and a partner *garantstelling* — is the single clearest piece of evidence that the penvoerder layer is thin, personally/organisationally dependent, and not redundant. Continuity was restored by concentrating further, not by distributing.
- **[I]** Accountability is real but procedural: quarterly reports to the Regioraad, a financial and substantive jaarverslag, SiSa to the Rijk, and unspent money returned. What is *not* evidenced is outcome accountability for the subsidised programmes. The monitor that would supply it disclaims causal attribution and, for one of nine themes, did nothing in 2025.
