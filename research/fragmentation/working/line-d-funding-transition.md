# Line D — the 2026→2027 funding transition, read at source

Lead retrieval, 2026-11-26T21:09–21:11Z. This file exists because the v1 note's central money claim (the "cliff" from €4,229,000 in 2026 to €739,000 in 2027) was carried from a December 2025 municipal projection that predates the successor instrument. The revision required reconciling the two. Everything below is retrieved and read by the Lead in this session unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Why this line was opened

Mandate: *"Reconcile the reported 2026–2027 municipal budget reduction with the successor AZWA/HLO funding arrangements. Distinguish currently committed funding, expiring instruments, prospective allocations and actual expenditure. Do not present an incomplete budget transition as an established reduction in future prevention capacity."*

The v1 note used the €4,229,000 → €739,000 movement as documentary support for "the money is temporary and the coordination has no long-term home". The movement is real in the document. What the document does *not* say — and what v1 did not check — is whether the 2027 figure is a like-for-like successor of the 2026 figure. It is not: 2026 includes a one-off national envelope; 2027 was written before the successor envelope existed.

## 2. What v1 relied on, and its exact wording

`EIND` — Gemeente Eindhoven, *Evaluatie 2024-2025 en Uitvoeringsagenda Programma Gezondheid 2026-2027*, December 2025, §D Financiën. Re-retrieved and read in full this session (sha256 `b601c9e890285e8d…`, 92,899 chars extracted).

> "De verwachte lasten bedragen: Voor 2026: € 4.229.000 / Voor 2027: € 739.000"

> "Dekking voor 2026 is voorzien vanuit: Preventie en Gezondheid (taakveld 7.1): € 647.000; SPUK-GALA (grotendeels taakveld 7.1): € 3.582.000. Totaal voor 2026: € 4.229.000"

> "Dekking voor 2027 is voorzien vanuit: Preventie en Gezondheid (taakveld 7.1): € 739.000. Totaal voor 2027: € 739.000"

> "De dekking voor 2027 valt veel lager uit dan 2026 als gevolg van het **(mogelijk) wegvallen** van de incidentele SPUK GALA (2023 t/m 2026). Er zijn geen structurele verplichtingen ten laste van de SPUK-GALA die doorlopen na 2026."

Two things were under-read in v1. First, **"(mogelijk)"** — the document itself marks the fall-away as possible, not decided. Second, the cost paragraph scopes the 2026 figure: "…en alle GALA-enveloppen (**alleen voor 2026**)". The 2026 total is a year that contains a one-off national envelope; the 2027 total is the municipality's own line with no successor envelope assumed, because none had been decided when the document was written.

## 3. The successor instrument, read at source

`SPUKAZWA` — Ministerie van VWS, *Regeling van de Minister van Volksgezondheid, Welzijn en Sport van 14 juli 2026, kenmerk 4476325-1101043-DMO, houdende een specifieke uitkering voor AZWA- en HLO-doelen*, **Staatscourant 2026, 25830**, published 21-07-2026. Full text retrieved (88,029 chars). This is the legal text v1 listed as a PI access request and never read.

Operative features:

| Feature | Provision | Consequence for the fragmentation question |
|---|---|---|
| Addressee | Art. 2.1(1): regional allocation to **each mandaathouder**; art. 3.1(1): local allocation to **each gemeente**; art. 2.1(3): 2027 allocation to **each GGD** | Money reaches municipalities and GGDs only. There is no legal route to any other party except as a municipal contractor. |
| Horizon | Art. 2.1(1)/3.1(1): **2027, 2028, 2029**; art. 8.2(2): regeling expires **16 July 2030** | Three years, not one. The v1 "one-year bridge" reading is superseded for the regional layer. |
| Allocation | Ambtshalve; **amounts fixed in the regeling itself** (Bijlagen 1–4) | No application, no competition, no queue. The "closed door" for money is gone; what remains is a condition. |
| Condition | Art. 4.1: allocation only if the mandaathouder submits a **werkagenda** for the region by **1 March 2027**; art. 5.1(1): beschikking no earlier than **1 February 2027** | A planning condition, not an eligibility test of the applicant. Content is not substantively assessed (per VNG Q&A, §5 below). |
| Coordination | Art. 2.1(2)(c): "**regionale coördinatie van gemeenten in de samenwerkingsregio**" is a named funded activity of the regional allocation | Coordination now has a three-year funded line inside the instrument. This directly qualifies v1's "coordination is a project, not a service". |
| GGD | Art. 2.1(3) + Bijlage 2: direct 2027 allocation "voor versterking van de regionale kennis- en adviesfunctie en coördinatie van de ketenaanpakken" | One year direct; 2028–2029 amounts are to be agreed between VWS and VNG in Q1 2027 (art. 2.2 toelichting). |
| Carry-over | Art. 5.2: up to **70%** may move to the following year, to be spent by 2029 | Reduces year-end cliff behaviour; does not extend the horizon. |
| Accountability | Art. 7.1: SiSa under art. 17a Financiële-verhoudingswet; art. 6.1(1)(b): cooperation with AZWA monitoring; art. 6.1(3)(a): no double funding | Lighter than the 15-compartment SiSa regime; still accountability, still audit. |
| State aid / procurement | Toelichting, "Staatssteun": municipalities engaging third parties must comply with procurement and state-aid rules; "geen ongeoorloofde staatssteun … geen indirecte staatssteun" | A documented legal constraint on routing public money to informal groups. This is a *condition of the instrument*, not evidence of anyone's intent. |

## 4. The municipal side, checked against its own later budget

`TV2027` — Gemeente Eindhoven, *Totaaloverzicht technische vragen Begroting 2027*, raadsinformatie doc 17577339, answered 20 October 2026. Retrieved and read (219,963 chars; targeted extraction). Question 44, D66, on Volksgezondheid (taakveld 7.1):

> "De SPUK AZWA bestaat uit een regionaal deel en een lokaal deel. Eindhoven is mandaathoudende gemeente voor het regionale deel. En elke gemeente ontvangt afzonderlijk een lokale SPUK AZWA. **Beiden zijn niet opgenomen in de begroting 2027 t/m 2029.** Ten tijde van opstelling begroting was nog onduidelijk in hoeverre dit al opgenomen kon worden in deze begroting. Het financiële beeld (na toevoeging in de begroting): aan de lastenkant komt er een bedrag van **ca. 9,6 miljoen** bij (dit betreft AZWA-middelen regionaal, lokaal en nationaal programma leefbaarheid en veiligheid voor gebied Woensel Zuid). Dit geldt ook voor de batenkant. Hierdoor blijft het saldo gelijk."

This is the pivotal retrieval. It establishes four things at once:

1. The **October 2026** budget still does not contain the AZWA income — so the low 2027 figure is not a decision to spend less; it is a gap in the budget's completeness, stated by the municipality itself.
2. The municipality's own estimate of the addition is **≈ €9.6 million**, which matches the sum of the regeling's annexes for Eindhoven (€9,601,453) to within rounding. Two independent documents reconcile.
3. The addition is **budget-neutral**: "Dit geldt ook voor de batenkant. Hierdoor blijft het saldo gelijk." The money is passed through, not net new municipal spending capacity.
4. The pass-through character is decisive for interpretation: **€5.97m of the €9.6m is regional money the city administers on behalf of fifteen municipalities**, so the 2027 figure is not comparable to the 2026 figure of municipal *own* programme spending plus a GALA envelope.

`BEG2027` — Gemeente Eindhoven, *Raadsvoorstel Vaststellen Begroting 2027*, B&W 6 October 2026, doc 17508427. Read in full (12,015 chars). Total budget €1.5bn; programme 2c Sociale stad €646m. It contains no AZWA line, consistent with `TV2027`. It is not evidence of a prevention cut; it is evidence that the successor money had not been booked.

## 6. What this establishes, and what it does not

**Established (S1, documented arrangement):**
- The successor instrument exists, is legally in force, is ambtshalve, and its per-region and per-municipality amounts are published in the regeling.
- For 2027, the region's allocation is €5,968,460, Eindhoven's local allocation is €3,103,887, the NPLV addition is €529,106, and the GGD's direct allocation is €155,110.
- The municipality's own October 2026 budget answer puts the missing addition at ≈€9.6m and states it is budget-neutral.
- Regional coordination and GGD coordination are named, funded activities of the new instrument.
- Money under the instrument reaches municipalities and GGDs; state-aid and procurement rules govern onward funding of third parties.

**Established (S2, reported experience):** the VNG states that outside the four named ketenaanpakken no structural GALA money remains, and that structural continuation beyond 2029 depends on evidence and a cabinet decision around 2029/2030.

**Corrected in v1:** "The municipal prevention budget falls from €4,229,000 (2026) to €739,000 (2027)" describes a **budget composition at a point before the successor allocation was known and booked**, not an established reduction in prevention capacity. The like-for-like comparison is 2026 SPUK-GALA for Eindhoven (€3,582,000) against 2027 local AZWA/HLO (€3,103,887 + €529,106 NPLV = €3,632,993): approximately flat, with a different composition and different conditions. The regional €5,968,460 is additional resource administered by the city on behalf of fifteen municipalities.

**Not established:**
- Whether any specific prevention activity loses funding. The instruments differ in scope: HLO (mantelzorg, respijtzorg) is added; roken/alcohol/drugs and other GALA themes are not named among the four continued ketenaanpakken. A composition change with winners and losers is plausible; no document retrieved quantifies it per theme. **[U]**
- Actual expenditure under the new instrument. It begins 1 February 2027; nothing has been spent at the retrieval date.
- Whether the region's werkagenda is adopted. No Zuid-Oost-Brabant werkagenda was found in the public record at retrieval (searches recorded in the note's access log). The 8 December 2026 deadline had not passed.
- Whether the €9.6m is realised at that level. It is the municipality's own projection against the regeling's annexes; two documents agreeing is corroboration of the *estimate*, not observation of the *transfer*.

**Why the v1 reading was tempting and wrong.** The December 2025 document is an honest document. It compares a year with a national envelope to a year without one, and it says "(mogelijk)". A report reading only that document, plus a national narrative about temporary money, produces a cliff. The cliff is in the accounting; the capacity question needs the successor instrument, which was published seven months later and which nobody in this research had read.
