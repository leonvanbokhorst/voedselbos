## 7. Note on the evidence hierarchy in this report

The strongest evidence in this note is documentary and locally dated: rules, budgets, awards, board papers, dates and the region's own 2026 diagnosis. The next strongest is national policy, where the MTR and the amendment are read in full and the 2026 RIVM report is abstract-only. The weakest is the scholarly literature, which is largely abstract-level and mostly not re-retrieved by the Lead. The note's argument is built so that the local and national documentary findings carry the answer, and the literature carries only the *conditions* and *mechanism plausibility* — never a pivotal local claim. Where a reader disagrees with the literature's weight, the local findings survive.
