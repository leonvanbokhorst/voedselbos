# Working notes — raw material for [`../note.md`](../note.md)

These four files are the raw output of the three delegated research lines for issue #19 plus the Lead's own transcription of the anchor case. They are kept for traceability, **not** as evidence. [`../note.md`](../note.md) is the deliverable; where the note and a working file disagree, the note governs, and where the note relies on an item it says at what depth that item was read.

| File | Line | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `lead-anchor.md` | Lead | Transcription of the anchor case's own pages, the Supportpunt portrait, the Trefpunt Groen 2021 interview, the closure post and the November 2026 newsletter, with sha256 prefixes and exact Dutch quotations. All of it retrieved and read in full by the Lead. |
| `local-voices.md` | delegated line (voices) | 43-row quote table of documented participant and organiser statements, anchor case and comparators; synthesis; silences; access-boundary register. Every row labelled with speaker and evidence type. |
| `literature-meaning.md` | delegated line (literature) | Claim tables with design, N, country, reported magnitude and critique; a rival-interpretation section; dead ends. **All items are abstract-level or record-level**, with read status per row. Two apparent printing errors in published abstracts are quoted as printed and flagged. |
| `relational-counterevidence.md` | delegated line (relations) | Claim table on relational processes and counter-evidence; sections on care as pleasure vs obligation, belonging vs conformity, recognition as visibility with five falsifiers; access log. Full text where stated, abstract/record otherwise. |

**Mark conventions differ between these files.** The repository uses **[D]** documented, **[C]** claimed, **[I]** inferred, **[U]** not established. The delegated files use their own marks — `[FT]`/`[A]`/`[T]`/`[G]` for read depth and `[E]`/`[T]`/`[I]`/`[U]` for epistemic status, defined in each file's header. `local-voices.md` marks `[D]`/`[C]`/`[I]`/`[U]` in the repository's sense. Read the header of each file before citing from it.

**Delegation record.** Three bounded researchers ran in parallel on disjoint files; each was instructed to retrieve rather than recall, to mark read depth, to hunt contradictions, to return "not established" rather than fill gaps, and not to spawn further agents. The Lead verified four load-bearing citations against independent records (see `../note.md` §11), corrected one year (Veen et al. is 2016, not 2015), and did not independently verify the remaining abstract-level items — those are reported as what their authors state, not as checked findings.

**What is not here.** No stakeholder was contacted; no login, paywall or Cloudflare challenge was bypassed; no participant's private words appear. Anything quoted is published text.
