Archetypes: **(a) volunteer commons on borrowed land**; **(b) professionalised care enterprise with foundation governance** (Wasven, Phood, differing in whether care or commerce is the revenue base); **(c) public estate/project with an institutional operator** (Wielewaal, Victoriapark); **(d) advocacy intermediary** (TGE), holding the only formal city-wide network and the only paid coordination capacity.

## 5. Where shared care would strain

1. **Land tenure is the fail point, not volunteer energy.** The node that closed is the one that owned nothing.
2. **Liability.** Wasven and Phood carry volunteers and participants inside care-quality regimes; visiting volunteers are a different risk category. Sharing people is not symmetric.
3. **Rhythm.** One fixed weekly window (Voedselbos), two fixed Saturday shifts plus daily commercial opening (Wasven), weekday care programmes (Phood). A shared calendar is possible; shared *tasks* less so.
4. **Mandate.** TGE's is advisory only, and it represents an *achterban*, not these initiatives. Making it the ecosystem's coordinator would change what it is.
5. **Funders cut across.** BPD funds Phood while TGE routinely resists development pressure; ASML funds the Wielewaal centre. Shared funders are not shared interests.

## 6. What would need to be true

- **#3 Voedselbos ↔ Wielewaal as a relation:** that a municipal or Natuurmonumenten decision allocated a food-forest site on the estate, and that the operator wanted such a node. Neither is evidenced; [S11] is the only opening.
- **#2 co-presence → collaboration:** that two participants of the 2024 market did something together afterwards that left a trace. None found.
- **#13 Victoriapark partners:** that Design Academy, the Bibliotheek or TAC signed or programmed anything. Currently a possibility in a design document.
- **#5 Supportpunt as connective tissue:** that its client base overlaps the green initiatives. It serves Eindhoven foundations and associations, but no client list was found.
- **TGE as hub rather than gatekeeper:** that initiatives outside its *achterban* experience it as reachable. Its report claims a growing *achterban* and new contacts with neighbourhood green groups [S8]; the six named initiatives are not documented members.
