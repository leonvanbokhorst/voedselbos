# Line-level workings — issue #4 (complementarity and conflict)

Four files, two delegated lines. **These are workings, not findings.** Every claim that survived into [`../note.md`](../note.md) was re-read against the original by the Lead researcher before use; where a claim here is *not* in the note, that is usually because it could not be verified or did not bear on the question.

## What each line covered

### `line-a-revealed.md` and `line-a-sources.md` — line A, the Dutch money-and-positioning record

- **Covered:** Wasven's three ANBI publications (2025), the *Voornemen tot uitgifte van grond* Didam notices, the DAEB designation, the municipal Woo publication *Subsidies in Eindhoven – 2025* and its ceilings, the municipal green-subsidy scheme, Phood Farm's funding, and volunteer-recruitment records.
- **Produced the three findings that changed the note:** (1) the entire green budget-subsidy line is one organisation (Trefpunt, €232,000); (2) the Wmo day-activity ceiling is fully committed with both Wasven and Phood inside it — the only measured zero-sum in the corpus; (3) Wasven is a member of Trefpunt, so the public network map understates the network.
- **Did not cover:** Phood's accounts (none exist publicly — no ANBI, no jaarrekening); the DAEB amount or any rent figure (undisclosed); any refusal of a green organisation (none found); the volunteer demand side (`eindhovendoet.nl` returned a site-wide 403 and the route was abandoned, not bypassed).
- **Also produced, and carried into the note as a methodological warning:** the ANBI "Baten van subsidies €0" line for a foundation receiving €176,594 from the city. Do not read municipal dependence off a charity filing.

### `line-b-literature.md` and `line-b-sources.md` — line B, the international literature named in the issue

- **Covered:** coopetition, organisational ecology / niche theory and resource partitioning, nonprofit competition for volunteers/donors/legitimacy, resource dependence theory, stakeholder salience, and the stated-versus-revealed-preference distinction — each with a level label, the strongest counter-evidence, and an explicit test of what it can and cannot license at the scale of a seven-node unequal city network.
- **Outcome:** six sources retrieved, **none meta-analytic**; two of the three reviews report no effect sizes. One concept (stakeholder salience) could not be obtained by any legitimate route and was **dropped** rather than cited unread. The line's own conclusion — that the concepts supply vocabulary plus one falsifiable expectation, not predictions — is reproduced in the note's §10.6, including the one place where the literature *cuts against* the note's framing.
- **Did not cover:** any claim about Eindhoven; the organisational-category/field literature that would be needed to support "who speaks for the field" (not retrieved, so that claim stands as an observation plus a hypothesis, not a literature finding); any meta-analysis of coopetition or nonprofit-competition outcomes.

## How to read the marks in these files

`[D]` documented in a retrieved source · `[C]` the author's or actor's own claim · `[I]` the line's inference · `[U]` not established in the sources that line searched. Level labels in line B (`definitional` / `single study` / `review` / `meta-analytic`) are never upgraded. Verbatim quotations and full metadata are in the two `*-sources.md` files.
