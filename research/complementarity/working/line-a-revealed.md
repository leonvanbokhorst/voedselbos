# Line A — findings: the Dutch-language money-and-positioning record

**Line:** the money, land and staffing record of the principal green/care-farm organisations in Eindhoven, read for STATED vs REVEALED interest.
**Retrieval date:** 26 November 2026. **Sources:** A1–A27, numbered as in `line-a-sources.md`.
**Marks:** [D] documented in a retrieved source · [C] claimed by an actor (self-description) · [I] inference · [U] not established.

**Headline.** The Wasven and Phood Farm are not competing for a municipal *green* budget — no such budget for organisations exists in the published record. They are both inside the same **capped Wmo day-activity pot** (basisdagbesteding), which in 2025 was allocated to the euro with zero residual. The Wasven additionally holds, from 2026, a **DAEB designation for 2026–2035 with structural municipal funding** and **two sole-candidate ground awards** — incumbency conferred by the municipality in its own published words. The competition that the money record actually reveals is therefore not a contest over green subsidy but over (i) a fixed number of care/day-activity places, (ii) municipal land and the institutional status that goes with it, and (iii) a shared, broker-mediated volunteer supply.

## (a) What the money and staffing record shows

### A.a.1 Wasven — three foundations, one group

Structure [D, A1]: "Het Wasven is organisatorisch en financieel zelfstandig. De drie stichtingen vormen tezamen een fiscale eenheid voor de omzetbelasting. Financiële rapportage vindt plaats per stichting."

**Group income 2025, from the three ANBI statements** [D, A5+A6+A7; the group total is my addition, marked [I]]:

| Entity | Total baten 2025 | Dominant source | Reported subsidies | Reported gifts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stichting Wasvenboerderij | € 1,192,085 | Commercial turnover € 1,192,085 | € 0 | € 0 |
| Stichting Groendomein Wasven | € 414,097 | Turnover € 385,500 (rent from the two sister foundations + staff house) | € 0 | € 28,597 |
| Stichting Wasvenzorg | € 457,610 | "Opbrengsten zorgprestaties en maatschappelijke ondersteuning" € 457,610 | € 0 | € 0 |
| **Group total [I]** | **€ 2,063,792** | | **€ 0** | **€ 28,597** |

The composition is the finding. [D] The commercial arm is pure turnover ("Baten als tegenprestatie voor levering van producten en/of diensten (omzet) € 1.192.085"); the care arm is pure care revenue; the land-owning arm earns its income mainly by renting the farm to its two sister foundations. Cross-subsidy flows through internal rent and through loans: "Vanaf de opstart zijn leningen verstrekt aan stichting Wasvenboerderij en stichting Wasvenzorg … tegen een marktconforme, zakelijke rente en verkeren in afbouwfase." [D, A9]

**The Wmo dependence is stated by the foundation itself:** "Gezien de te verwachte marktschommelingen in deelnemers als gevolg van de WMO streeft de stichting naar een eigen vermogen van maximaal een halfjaaromzet." [D, A7] That single sentence locates the Wasven's real revenue risk in the municipal care market, not in the green domain.

**The municipal record says something the ANBI forms do not.** The municipality's own Woo publication of outgoing subsidies lists three 2025 grants to *Stichting Groendomein Wasven* [D, A22]:
- € 148,394 — "Organiseren indicatievrije basisdagbesteding Locatie Wasven" (Tabel 16, p.22)
- € 24,300 — "Programma Samen Waarderen" (Subsidieregeling Global Goals, Tabel 20, p.31)
- € 3,900 — "Organiseren Evenementen Wasven" (Subsidieregeling evenementen, Tabel 8, p.10)
- **Total € 176,594 for 2025 [I, addition]**

Yet all three ANBI forms report "Subsidies van overheden" as zero or blank. [D] The reconciliation is almost certainly presentational: the dagbesteding grant is booked as "Baten als tegenprestatie voor levering van producten en/of diensten (omzet)" — a purchase of a service rather than a subsidy. [I] **This matters methodologically for the whole study: the ANBI subsidy line is not a measure of municipal dependence.** A researcher who read only the ANBI forms would conclude the Wasven takes no public money; the municipality's own publication shows €176,594 in 2025, plus an unquantified DAEB stream from 2026.

**The DAEB designation is the largest single fact in this line** [D, A20, B&W 10 March 2026]: "Het college van burgemeester en wethouders heeft besloten het ecologisch beheer van de gemeentelijke gronden van Groendomein Wasven, in samenhang met de daaraan functioneel verbonden collectieve activiteiten van inwoners, aan te wijzen als Dienst van Algemeen Economisch Belang (DAEB) voor de periode 2026–2035." With: "Voor de uitvoering van de DAEB-taken stelt het college structureel middelen beschikbaar binnen de gemeentelijke begroting." And: "Er is geen besluitvorming door de raad vereist."

The stated purpose is explicitly to *lock in* an existing position: "Met dit besluit wordt een bestaande samenwerking juridisch en bestuurlijk geborgd." The DAEB route is the state-aid-compliant way to fund a chosen provider without tendering: "De DAEB-aanwijzing maakt het mogelijk deze publieke taken rechtmatig te ondersteunen binnen het Europese staatssteunkader." [D] **The amount is not stated anywhere in the document** [U].

**Staffing** [D, A5–A7]: 15 fte paid staff (Wasvenboerderij), 0 fte (Groendomein), 0 fte (Wasvenzorg); 30 regular volunteers (Wasvenboerderij) and 150 (Groendomein) — 180 in total [I, addition]. As a *volunteer* mobiliser the Wasven is an order of magnitude larger than as an employer: ~180 regular volunteers against ~15 fte.

There is an internal inconsistency in the ANBI forms that limits their reliability on staffing [D figures; I the conclusion]: Groendomein reports 0 paid fte yet €136,548 of personnel costs; Wasvenzorg reports 0 paid fte yet €353,388 of personnel costs. Either staff are employed by one entity and recharged, or the "aantal medewerkers" field is completed carelessly. A second, smaller inconsistency: Groendomein's 2025 note says "De baten en lasten zijn in lijn met voorgaande jaren" while the same table shows a swing from −€40,790 (2024) to +€13,691 (2025). [D]

[D] There is no document with notes to the accounts, no segment split of the €1.19m turnover (gasterij vs shop vs bakery vs tuinderij), and no breakdown of the care revenue by funder. [U]

### A.a.2 Wasven — land and the municipality

This is where the record is strongest, and where the brief's premise needs correcting.

- The brief's premises, "**~24 ha municipal ground**" — **not established** [U]. No retrieved document states a total area. The two published parcels are **7,306 m²** and **9,355 m²** — together ~1.67 ha [I].
- The legal instrument is **not erfpacht and not a single lease**. Three distinct instruments appear [D]:
  1. **Beheerovereenkomst** with the municipality for buildings and green: "het beheren en onderhouden van de opstallen en het groen conform de beheerovereenkomst met de gemeente Eindhoven" (A6, A9).
  2. **Huurovereenkomst**, 15 years with extension option, for ~7,306 m², perceel Tongelre sectie D nr. 712, "gelegen tegenover Celebeslaan 30", for a solar field plus agroforestry (A17, 3 March 2025).
  3. **Gebruiksovereenkomst** for ~9,355 m², perceel Tongelre sectie D nr. 1121 (part), "gelegen in het verlengde van de Ceramlaan", in the Warande (A19, rectification of A18, 15/20 January 2026).
- The land is municipally owned: the DAEB raadsbrief calls it "de gemeentelijke gronden van Groendomein Wasven" [D, A20]. Against this, the Wasven's own beleidsplan calls Groendomein "eigenaar en beheerder van het groendomein Wasven" [C, A9]. **Reconciliation [I]:** "eigenaar" refers to the buildings/opstallen (materiële vaste activa €1,473,904; langlopende schulden €1,202,413), while the ground is municipal and held under lease/use agreements. The two statements are compatible, but the Wasven's wording is the more generous one and should not be read as landownership.

## (b) Evidence of competition for the same money

### A.b.1 A hard ceiling, fully allocated
The basisdagbesteding regeling has a published ceiling and it was exhausted [D, A22, Tabel 38, p.49]:
> "Basisdagbesteding volwassenen | Plafond 2025 € 5.272.000 | Subsidie 2025 € 5.272.000 | Restant € 0"

Tabel 16 (pp.22–23) lists **38 locations** [D, counted; the 38 rows sum to €5,272,003 against the stated €5,272,000 — a €3 rounding difference, which is how the publication's own totals behave]. Both organisations are rows in that single table: the Wasven at €148,394 (2.81% of the pot) and Phood Farm at €71,440 across two locations (1.36%) [I, shares computed]. The ceiling being at zero residual is the clearest documented instance of a closed, zero-sum allocation in which both appear. [D ceiling; I its competitive significance]

**What this is not:** it is not a tender, and no refusal is published. The publication states the process — "Na de beoordeling van de aanvraag … wordt de aangevraagde subsidie (gedeeltelijk) toegekend of afgewezen" [D, A22] — but **no rejected applications are published anywhere** [U]. So we can document the ceiling and the incumbents; we cannot document a refusal, a waiting list or a displaced applicant.

### A.b.2 There is no municipal green money for organisations to compete for
This is the most consequential negative finding, and it is well evidenced [D, A22]. In the complete Woo publication of 2025 outgoing subsidies:
- The **only** green-sector budget subsidy in the entire city is one line, to the peak body: "Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven | Netwerkorganisatie voor gemeente Eindhoven en haar inwoners om de lokale groene leefomgeving te bewaken en te versterken | 232.000" (Tabel 5, p.7). It is the sole entry under "Begrotingssubsidies Ruimtelijk domein", whose total is €232,000.
- The only *regeling* under Ruimtelijk domein is "Subsidieregeling sloop brommers en scooters" (€800).
- Full-text search for "natuur", "milieu", "landschap", "groen" and "voedselbos" returns no other recipient organisation.

The municipality's "groensubsidie" is a **private-property** scheme, not an initiative fund [D, A16]: "Ben je eigenaar van een woning of (bedrijfs)pand van minimaal 5 jaar oud? Dan kun je een subsidie krijgen" — with per-m² rates and per-applicant ceilings (€5/m² to max €2,500; €25/m² to max €25,000; €10/m² to max €10,000). No recipients are published, and the scheme is not addressed to organisations running green or community initiatives. [D]

**Implication for the main question:** "competition for green grants" among Eindhoven green initiatives is, on this record, largely a non-event — because the grant category does not exist. The municipal green relationship is instead institutionalised in *one* body (TGE) plus *one* favoured provider (the Wasven). Competition, where it exists, is for care places, land, volunteers and visibility.

### A.b.3 Incumbency by direct award (Didam), twice
The municipality awarded ground to Groendomein Wasven without a selection procedure, twice in twelve months, and published its reasons — legally required after the Didam judgment [D, A19]:
> "Gelet op het arrest van de Hoge Raad van 26 november 2021, ECLI:NL:HR:2021:1778 (het zogenoemde Didam-arrest) en de daaruit voortvloeiende verplichting voor gemeenten om, ter waarborging van het gelijkheidsbeginsel, voorafgaand aan een gronduitgifte openbaar te motiveren waarom een selectieprocedure wel of niet wordt gehouden."

The reasoning is a textbook statement of incumbency [D]:
> "Stichting Groendomein Wasven beheert de omringende gronden reeds langdurig … De stichting is daarmee de natuurlijke voortzetter van het gebruik en waarborgt de continuïteit van beheer." (ground 1)
> "Het inschakelen van een alternatieve gegadigde zou leiden tot fragmentatie van het beheer, verlies van samenhang en afbreuk doen aan de maatschappelijke meerwaarde." (ground 5)
> "Op basis van deze argumenten beschouwt de gemeente stichting Groendomein Wasven, volgens objectieve en redelijke criteria, als de enige geschikte kandidaat voor de uitgifte van de grond." (A17)

Each notice carries a 28-day window to bring summary proceedings ("kort geding") at the Rechtbank Oost-Brabant; after it, "vervalt het recht om tegen deze voorgenomen uitgifte van de grond op te komen" [D]. **Whether anyone used that window is not established** [U] — and a challenge would be the single best piece of evidence for the competitive reading, so its absence from the record is a real gap, not a null.

Note also the record-keeping quality: the January 2026 notice was published incomplete ("De tekst in de kennisgeving was niet compleet", A19) and had to be rectified four days later, and the rectification dates the underlying college decision "13 januari 2025" while publishing in January 2026 [D]. I record the discrepancy without resolving it [I: probably a typographical error].

### A.b.4 The strongest incumbency instrument: DAEB
See §a.1. A ten-year designation, structural funding inside the budget, state-aid-compliant, no council vote required. [D, A20] In competitive terms this converts a grant relationship into a utility-style mandate. Nothing in the record shows any other Eindhoven green organisation holding, or being considered for, a DAEB designation [U — absence across all retrieved sources].

### A.b.5 One institutional voice, subsidised
The municipality funds and formalises a single representative body [D, A21]:
> "TGE speelt als koepelorganisatie van groen en milieubelangen in de stad een belangrijke rol in de gemeentelijke democratie, brengt zaken die bij burgers spelen in de stad gecoördineerd naar voren richting gemeente … **Jaarlijks ontvangt TGE subsidie van de gemeente op basis van een ingediend werkplan.**"

And the founding purpose is stated with unusual frankness [D, A21]:
> "het kwalitatief verbeteren van voorliggende plannen op het gebied van groen en natuur, het vergroten van het maatschappelijk draagvlak en de kans op bezwaarprocedures verkleinen."

For "who speaks for green Eindhoven": the municipality has constituted one interlocutor (€232,000/year) whose mandate includes *reducing the chance of objections*. That is a documented municipal interest in a single, cooperative voice. Individual initiatives — including the Wasven — are members within it, not voices of it. [D the arrangement; I the consequence]

## (c) Evidence of complementarity (the counter-evidence, reported in full)

This line found real, documented complementarity and it should not be suppressed in favour of the competitive reading.

1. **The Wasven is a member of the collective green structure, by its own account** [C, A12]: "Bij Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven zijn ruim honderd groepen aangesloten, waaronder het Wasven. Al sinds de oprichting in 2002 werken we samen om de kwaliteit van de leefomgeving te verbeteren." Corroborated in substance by the municipality's description of TGE as the koepel of a large number of member associations [D, A21]. Independent confirmation *from TGE's side* that the Wasven is a member was not retrieved [U].
2. **Both organisations deliver the same public instrument, side by side, without any evidence of displacing each other** [D, A22]. Sharing a funding instrument is the opposite of a zero-sum scramble when both are already funded: the 2025 allocation was settled, with both inside it.
3. **The Wasven's statutes name collaboration as an activity, not just a slogan** [D, A6]: "het aangaan van wederkerige relaties en samenwerking met leveranciers, vrijwilligers en lokale organisaties waardoor sociale netwerken worden versterkt" — and its income policy is written in terms of "maatschappelijke exploitatie", not market share.
4. **Documented 2025 co-programming** [D, A8]: stichting Wereldboom (tree climbing / veteran trees during Dutch Design Week), Get Running (the Wasvenloop half-marathon through the Dommel valley), TU/e students (sustainable business models), the TV programme Binnenste Buiten, and NH1816 plus local insurers funding free tickets for participants and volunteers.
5. **A shared, brokered volunteer channel rather than a poached one** [D]: the Wasven works through Samen voor Eindhoven — "Het Wasven is één van de maatschappelijke partners waar medewerkers van bedrijven kunnen meehelpen op onze akkers of in het groen … onder de noemer 'Groene Daden' of door deel te nemen aan de jaarlijkse NL Doet of de Natuurwerkdag" (A13) — while Phood Farm lists "Eindhoven Doet & Alle vrijwilligers" among its partners (A24). Both brokers are themselves municipal grant recipients in 2025: Samen voor Eindhoven €104,328 ("Matchen/uitvoeren van hulpvragers met bedrijfsvrijwilligers") and Eindhoven Doet, under Stichting Lumens, €584,172 (A22, Tabel 32). The volunteer market is thus mediated by subsidised infrastructure rather than left to direct rivalry [D the grants; I the mediating effect].
6. **No document retrieved contains a single disparaging or comparative claim by either organisation about the other** [D — negative result from full texts of A8, A12, A13, A23, A24, A25, A26]. The Wasven's 2025 annual overview names a sports club, an insurer, a tree foundation, a university and a broadcaster as its collaborators — no peer green initiative appears, but neither is any framed as a rival [D].

**Where a genuine positioning claim does appear** [C]: Phood Farm describes itself in the definite article — "Phood Farm is **dé** urban farm die de stad inspireert" (A23) — and the Wasven brands itself "Wij zijn het Wasven. Groen in alles wat we doen" with its landscape "Onderdeel van natuurwerk Brabant" (A14). These are claims to primacy and to belonging, respectively. They are positioning, not evidence of conflict.

**One notable absence from the Wasven's "partners" page** [D, A11]: of the seventeen partner pages, almost all are *certifications and memberships* (EKO, Green Key, Skal, Ongehinderd, Kwaliteit laat je zien, Governancecode Zorg, ANBI, SDG, Social Return, Erkend Leerbedrijf, Kern met Pit, Geluksplek, Goed Geregeld) plus three network affiliations (Trefpunt Groen, Samen voor Eindhoven, Wereldboom). "Partners" in Wasven usage mostly means *keurmerken*, not peer organisations. Any inference that a rich partner ecosystem implies dense collaboration with other initiatives would be wrong.
