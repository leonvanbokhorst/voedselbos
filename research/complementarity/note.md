# Where do the initiatives genuinely complement each other, and where do their interests collide?

**Issue:** [#4](https://github.com/leonvanbokhorst/voedselbos/issues/4) · **Branch:** `research/4-complementarity-conflict` · **Status:** provisional throughout. No claim here is admitted; admission is the reviewer's act under the repository's provenance practice.
**Date:** desk research, 26 November 2026. Public sources only. No stakeholder contact, no fieldwork, no mediation, no partnership brokering, no ranking of initiatives, no implementation design.
**Lens declared:** **stated versus revealed interests** — what each initiative says about collaboration and community, set against what its funding, land, staffing, partnership list and public positioning show it actually receives and allocates.
**The lens's own limit, stated up front and not buried:** the retrieved literature that defines the distinction treats institutions as exactly the kind of entity to which preferences are *not* attributed — "those agents who do not — e.g. machines, plants or institutions" (line B §6, quoting the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* on preferences). "Revealed" in this note therefore means **what a decision allocated**, never **what an organisation wants**. Where the text says "prioritised", read "allocated to".
**Marks:** **[D]** documented in a retrieved source · **[C]** claimed by one actor or self-description · **[I]** inference of this note · **[U]** not established in the sources searched · **[P]** commissioner-provided. Source ids `R1`–`R22` resolve in [`sources.md`](sources.md); merged sibling notes are cross-referenced as #1 (network), #20 (local practices), #3 (participation/exhaustion), #17 (visibility) and #27 (health fragmentation) rather than re-cited.

**This note is not a proposal.** The issue asks which domains are safe to share, which need negotiation and which should stay separate. That reading is given in §7.3 and is marked **[I]** throughout: it is what the record's allocations imply, not an offer of brokering. No party has been asked anything.

## 1. The answer in short

1. **The prize in this field is not a grant. It is a secured position: a lease, a designation, a maintenance contract — and that competition has already been decided, in writing, in favour of the incumbent.** Three verified municipal decisions make the shape explicit. A **fifteen-year lease**, published as a *voornemen tot uitgifte* — an intention to grant, taking effect only "na het verkrijgen van de Omgevingsvergunning" — on 7,306 m² of municipal ground at Celebeslaan 30 to Stichting Groendomein Wasven, single-candidate, because the foundation "is de natuurlijke voortzetter van het gebruik" and it is "niet realistisch om een andere partij aan te wijzen" **[D R22]**. A further parcel in the Warande, single-candidate, where admitting another candidate "zou leiden tot fragmentatie van het beheer" **[D R4]**. And a **ten-year DAEB designation (2026–2035)** with "structureel middelen" from the municipal budget, made by the college with "geen besluitvorming door de raad vereist" **[D R20]**. Meanwhile the food forest held 0.7 ha **[P]** of private ground **[D R8]** with no written term **[U]**, and closed; the commissioner reports the land was sold **[P]**. This is not a rivalry that was lost; it is a category that was never in the running.

2. **The one place where two initiatives in this set demonstrably sit in the same, fully-committed budget is the Wmo day-activity pot — not the green budget.** The *basisdagbesteding volwassenen* ceiling for 2025 is **€5,272,000, allocated €5,272,000, remainder €0**; inside it are **Stichting Groendomein Wasven (€148,394)** and **Stichting Phood Farm (€22,450 + €48,990)** **[D R18]**. That is measurable, near-zero-sum competition: at the margin, one organisation's additional places must be accommodated under a ceiling that is already fully spent. It is competition *for care participants and for the municipal day-activity contract*, in which the green identity of the two organisations is incidental. **Whether any award was ever reduced to make room for another is [U]** — the record shows the structure, not an instance.

3. **There is no competitive green money at all: the city's entire green budget-subsidy line is one organisation.** In the municipality's own Woo publication of €152 m of outgoing subsidies, the *Ruimtelijk Domein* sector totals **€232,800**: **€232,000** of budget subsidies, whose single row is **Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven, €232,000**, described by the city as a "**Netwerkorganisatie** voor gemeente Eindhoven en haar inwoners om de lokale groene leefomgeving te bewaken en te versterken", plus **€800** of subsidy schemes, whose recipient is not an organisation at all — Tabel 15 shows "Natuurlijke personen (2 personen x € 400) | Tegemoetkoming in de kosten voor het laten slopen van oude scooters en brommers" **[D R18]**. So the entire sector consists of one organisational subsidy, one advisory contract, and two people who scrapped a scooter. Trefpunt's published turnover is also €232,000, to the euro **[D R1]**. Against that, the city has committed **€600,000 a year** to maintaining one estate and **€9.8 m** to its opening programme **[D R5]**. The city thus spends roughly 2.6 times more each year maintaining a single estate than on its only green civil-society subsidy line **[D for both figures; I for the comparison]**. **No green initiative is competing with another for green subsidy, because there is one line and one recipient** **[D]**. The comparison with €600,000 is the note's, not the city's.

4. **Correction to the intuitive reading, and the strongest counter-evidence to finding 3 above: the volunteer pot is open, has money left, accepts applications all year, and no green initiative is in it.** The *Versterken sociale basis voor vrijwilligers* scheme had a 2025 ceiling of €1,416,716 of which **€128,035 remained unallocated**, and the wider *Versterken Sociale Basis* left **€1,485,299** **[D R18]**. Categorie B explicitly takes one-off applications "het hele jaar door" up to **€15,000**, from "vrijwilligersorganisaties en natuurlijke personen" **[D R18]**. The recipient list contains scouting groups, minority self-organisations, elderly associations and kinderkamp organisers — **no garden, food forest or green group of any kind** **[D R18]**. So the mechanism is **non-application, not exclusion**, and it is the same mechanism the fragmentation note reached from the other direction (#27). This finding weakens the "competition for grants" reading and strengthens the "portfolio artefact" reading (§10.2).

5. **The documented complementarity is real, thin, and always hosted: one party owns the venue, the stage, the mandate or the membership, and the others appear on it.** Five documented instances: eight Groen Cafés in 2025 co-delivered by Trefpunt with Bibliotheek Eindhoven, KNNV, IVN and Wasven **[D R1]**; a green market on Wasven's erf at DDW 2024 where the food forest sold its syrups beside thirteen other organisations **[D R7]**; Trefpunt's advisory engagement on the estate's opening, restaurant conversion and nature centre **[D R1]**; Wasven's "Kleine Wildernis" with a secondary school and IVN **[D R14]**; and — new here, and the most consequential — **Wasven is a member of Trefpunt Groen**, on Wasven's own account: "Bij Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven zijn ruim honderd groepen aangesloten, waaronder het Wasven" **[D R19]**, at `https://wasven.nl/partners/trefpunt-groen-eindhoven/`. **This edge is not in the merged network note (#1)** and should be reconciled there rather than re-researched. This **extends** BRIEF §1.3 ("co-presence, not exchange") by naming what the co-presence is: **audience aggregation, advice and affiliation** — the three forms of collaboration that cost the host least and confer most standing on it.

6. **Two corrections to the merged research and to my own first draft, both from the same page.** Trefpunt claims **"ruim honderd groepen"** affiliated **[C R19]**, so its public network map's six rendered nodes **[D R2]** is a **JavaScript rendering artefact, not the size of the network**, and any statement that the network is "six nodes" must be withdrawn. And Wasven — the city's largest green landholder — is *inside* that membership. The connectivity is therefore greater than the earlier reading, and it runs **upwards into an advocacy body**, not sideways between practices.

7. **The deepest documented volunteer base in the city sits inside the most institutionally secured organisation.** Stichting Groendomein Wasven's statutory filing reports **0 fte paid staff and 150 volunteers** working more than three times a year **[D R21]**; its homepage advertises named volunteer roles including a coffee roaster and a day-activity driver alongside a paid 24-hour care vacancy **[D R13]**; it runs three recurring volunteer mornings a month **[D R13]**. The food forest's volunteer apparatus was one weekly window and a market stall **[D R7]**. **In Eindhoven the volunteer commons does not compete with institutions; it is what institutions are made of** **[D/I]** — the largest volunteer body in this corpus is an ANBI foundation with a DAEB designation.

8. **"Who speaks for green Eindhoven" is allocated — by legal form, statutorily defined scale and payroll — and no practice holds a channel.** In spatial policy the voice is Trefpunt's: "de stem van de natuur te vertegenwoordigen", 53 *binding* felling advices, a two-weekly municipal alignment meeting, a four-plus-five Centraal Milieu Overleg, and the city's own description of it as *the* network organisation **[D R1, R3, R18]**. In the regional health movement the green voice is Wasven's — one of 193 DE STAP partners **[D R17]** — while Phood Farm, Trefpunt, and every garden are absent from that list **[D R17]**. The two voices differ in **scale by statute**: Wasven's object is the social cohesion "en identiteit van **Tongelre**" **[D R21]**, Trefpunt's mandate is the city **[D R18]**. In neither field does the volunteer commons hold a channel **[D/I]**.

9. **The sharpest conflict in the record is not between initiatives but inside the field's self-description.** The closing initiative's November 2026 newsletter states "**Eindhoven heeft géén voedselbos meer**" **[D R9]**, eleven weeks after a local news report documented a residents' food forest in het Henri Dunantpark — also absent from the national open-day map **[D R8]**. One node's claim to speak for the movement renders another node invisible. Nobody gains a subsidy or a hectare from this; what is at stake is the definition of the field. The claim is also understandable, because the two are different in form: "niet een uitgebreid activiteitenprogramma op particulier terrein, maar een groen bewonersinitiatief in een stadspark" **[D R8, the journalist's framing]**. Whether the sentence was carelessness or a defensible distinction is **[U]**.

10. **Asymmetry, not disagreement, is the dominant fact — and the numbers are now measured, not asserted.** On the secured side: a ten-year DAEB, a fifteen-year lease, €9.8 m of capital, €600 k a year of maintenance, €5.27 m of committed care budget, €2.06 m of group turnover **[D R20, R22, R5, R18, R21/line A]**. On the other side: 0.7 ha of borrowed private ground with one coordinator that has closed, and gardens of 100 m² to 1,800 m² riding on four different hosts **[D R8; #20]**. Stated ambition exceeds revealed allocation in every node where both are documented, and the gap is always in the same place: the community and volunteer dimension (§8). This is a claim about **allocation**, not sincerity — see §10.1.

## 5. Where interests genuinely coincide

Six coincidences are documented. Each is real; each is also cheap for the party holding the asset.

1. **A stage.** Eight Groen Cafés in 2025 co-delivered with Bibliotheek Eindhoven, KNNV Eindhoven, IVN and Wasven; theme range from biodiversity and health to water, agroforestry and regenerative agriculture **[D R1]**. What the partnership consists of is a shared audience and programme; nothing in the report shows shared cost, obligation or decision.
2. **A venue.** The food forest sold its products at "Wasven Natuurlijk" on Wasven's erf, on a theme explicitly about making green volunteering attractive — "Het accent ligt op het laten zien wat vrijwilligerswerk in het groen zo leuk, boeiend en zinvol maakt" **[D R7]**. Wasven supplied ground, buildings, catering and audience; the food forest supplied product and stand.
3. **A mandate.** Trefpunt used its planning channel on the estate next door: *Wielewachters* sessions, concerns on ecological values, traffic concerns on the landhuis-to-restaurant conversion, support for a nature centre in the tuinmanswoning **[D R1]**. The estate got an independent ecological reading; Trefpunt got standing at the city's flagship.
4. **A field-level division of voice, and of scale.** Spatial policy → Trefpunt, city-wide by mandate **[D R18]**. Regional health → Wasven, one of 193 partners **[D R17]**. The mandates do not overlap, and each organisation is in the field where its form fits.
5. **A membership.** Wasven is a member of Trefpunt Groen, "Al sinds de oprichting in 2002" **[D R19]**. The city's largest green landholder is inside the city's green advocacy body. This is the most substantive affiliation in the corpus, and it was found on the member's page, not the body's.
6. **An adversary.** Interests converge against development pressure on the green structure, which Trefpunt documents as cumulative and severe: "Iedere individuele ontwikkeling lijkt acceptabel, maar bij elkaar opgeteld zijn de ontwikkelingen desastreus voor de natuurfuncties" **[D R1]**. No other initiative is recorded as taking a position **[U]**.

**What is not among the coincidences.** No shared land, no shared employment, no shared insurance or liability, no joint funding application, no shared roster, no joint legal person, and no document in which two initiatives commit each other to anything **[U across R1–R22 and #1]**. The coincidence is in the *audience*, the *argument* and the *membership list* — never in the *obligation*.

## 6. Where they compete

For each domain: what is documented, what is inferred, what is not established. **No source in this corpus has one initiative describing another as a competitor.** That absence is a property of the genres (promotional, institutional, journalistic) and not evidence of harmony (§10.3).

### 6.1 Secured position — the strongest, and already decided

- **Three verified awards to one organisation, all on incumbency grounds.** A 15-year lease on 7,306 m², single candidate, "natuurlijke voortzetter", "niet realistisch om een andere partij aan te wijzen" **[D R22]**; a Warande parcel, single candidate, anti-fragmentation reasoning **[D R4]**; a DAEB designation for 2026–2035 with structural money, college-only **[D R20]**. The award notices even name the legal instrument for objecting: a kort geding within 28 days **[D R22, R4]**.
- **What a volunteer commons faces instead.** Community management of medium/high-value municipal green "wordt afgewezen" **[D R11 art. 10(2)]**; sale of even basis-value green is refused where "het verzoek … gedaan wordt met doel de grond als tuin in gebruik te nemen", and leasing follows the same rules **[D R11 art. 11(1)(b), 11(5)]**; the resident green subsidy is €2,500, owner-only, physical measures **[D R12]**; green-compensation money is developer money and is barred inside the *groene wiggen* **[D R11 art. 7]**.
- **The conclusion this licenses, and the one it does not.** It licenses: *the land competition is decided before any volunteer group could enter it, and the deciding criterion is continuity of the sitting manager*. It does **not** license: *the municipality refused the food forest* — no refusal document exists in the record, and the food forest's ground was private **[D R8]**. **The competition is with the category, not with a rival applicant [D/I].**

### 6.2 Money — one zero-sum pot, and it is not green

- **The measured zero-sum.** *Basisdagbesteding volwassenen*: ceiling €5,272,000, allocated €5,272,000, **remainder €0**; recipients include **Wasven €148,394** and **Phood €22,450 + €48,990** **[D R18]**. Two organisations in this matrix draw from the same exhausted ceiling. Growth by one is, at the margin, accommodation by the others **[I]**; a documented instance of a trade-off is **[U]**.
- **No green market exists.** The *Ruimtelijk Domein* sector totals €232,800 = **€232,000** of budget subsidies, whose single row is Trefpunt, **plus €800** of subsidy schemes paid to two natural persons under the scooter/brommer scrappage scheme (Tabel 15) **[D R18]**. Two organisations cannot compete for a line with one organisational recipient; the only other payment in the sector is not to an organisation at all.
- **The open door nobody used.** The volunteer scheme left €128,035 of €1,416,716 unspent, takes one-off applications up to €15,000 all year from organisations or natural persons, and contains no green recipient **[D R18]**. This is the counter-finding to any "competition for grants" story (§10.2).
- **The small-grant tier is populated by gardens** — a €5,000 Postcode Loterij Fonds record for Buurttuin vol Kleur, spring 2026 **(#20, #3)** — the tier where competition between *practices* is plausible **[I]**, though still undocumented.
- **A methodological trap, verified.** Stichting Groendomein Wasven's ANBI filing reports "Baten van subsidies **€0**" while the same legal entity received **€176,594** from the city in 2025 (€148,394 + €24,300 + €3,900) **[D R21 vs R18]**: the money is booked as *"baten als tegenprestatie voor levering van producten en/of diensten"*. **Municipal dependence cannot be read off charity filings**; revealed priority must be built from award documents.

### 6.3 Volunteers — genuinely shared, genuinely shrinking, asymetrically recruited

- **Scarcity stated by the municipality**: "het aantal Eindhovenaren dat zich vrijwilliger noemt neemt al jaren af terwijl de vraag naar vrijwilligers toeneemt" **[D R10]**.
- **Recruitment capacity is concentrated and documented**: Wasven — 150 regular volunteers in one foundation, three recurring mornings a month, named roles including a volunteer coffee roaster and a day-activity driver **[D R21, R13]**; De Wielewaal — a group budgeted inside the €7.6 m verfraaiing/branding line, intended to grow into "Vrienden van de Wielewaal" **[D R5, R6]**; Phood — via Eindhoven Doet **[D R16]**.
- **The food forest's apparatus** was one weekly window and a market stall **[D R7]**; the 100 m² Block Acker plot could not mobilise volunteers at all **(#20)**.
- **Not established:** that any individual was contested, or that any organisation changed a programme because another had taken volunteers **[U]**.

### 6.4 Visitors and audience

- **An overlap of offer is documented; a contest is not.** Two estate destinations now offer, in the same south-eastern green structure, a shop, coffee, waymarked walking, excursions and volunteers: the Wielewaal with "een winkel met natuurproducten en een koffiehoek", "excursies" and "kleinschalige evenementen" **[D R6]**; Wasven with a gasterij seven days and a winkel six days **[D R13]**, in Tongelre, one of "de acht groene gebieden die Tongelre rijk is" **[D R15]**.
- **No visitor numbers are published by any node [U]**. Without them "competition for visitors" is an inference from an overlap of offer and cannot be sized; a densifying city may be expanding the pool rather than dividing it **[I]**.
- **The offers also differ**: the Wielewaal sells a national NGO's nature experience and a Philips-landgoed story; Wasven sells care, its own produce and a working farmyard **[D R6, R13]**.

### 6.5 Media attention

- **A documented asymmetry, one instance each way, no measure.** The Wielewaal opening and nature centre were announced by the operator with the wethouder and ASML quoted **[D R6, R5]**. The food forest's closure was local news, and its absence from the national open-day map was the hook **[D R8]**. No coverage count was found or attempted **[U]**.

### 6.6 "Who speaks for green Eindhoven"

- **Two allocated voices at two statutory scales** **[D R18, R1, R3, R17, R21]**; within spatial policy the mandate is exclusive in practice, since the city *asks* Trefpunt and its felling advices bind **[D R1]**.
- **The commons holds no channel** in either field — absent from Trefpunt's public map (R2, a rendering), from the national food-forest map **[D R8]**, and from the 193 health partners **[D R17]**. Three absences in three fields is a pattern **[I]**, and each is an absence from a *published list*, not proof of exclusion.
- **The contested sentence**: "Eindhoven heeft géén voedselbos meer" **[D R9]** against a documented food forest in het Henri Dunantpark **[D R8]**. The only place in the record where a node asserts the field.

## 10. Counter-evidence and tensions

### 10.1 The collaboration rhetoric may be genuine, and probably is

The record contains five real joint undertakings, a signed municipal protocol and a membership relationship dating to 2002 **[D R1, R7, R14, R4, R3, R19]**. None is a press release about collaboration; each has a date, a programme or a document. It is entirely plausible that the people involved mean what they say about community, and that the thinness of reciprocity is a **capacity** fact — too few paid hours, too much regulation, a shrinking volunteer base **[D R10]** — rather than an **interest** fact. If so, the stated-versus-revealed lens is measuring constraint, not preference. This note cannot distinguish the two, and the retrieved literature is explicit that behaviour under constraint does not identify preference **(line B §6)**.

### 10.2 The competition may be a municipal-portfolio artefact — and here the evidence is genuinely mixed

**For the artefact reading:** the instruments are categorical (owner-only subsidy, green-value thresholds, exclusive DAEB designations, day-activity ceilings); where a genuine competitive allocation exists for volunteers, it was **not exhausted** and **no green organisation applied** **[D R18]**; where green ground is allocated, the criterion is continuity of the sitting manager **[D R22, R4]**. Under this reading there is no market in which these organisations meet as rivals — they meet in the city's filing system, and the "collision" is an artefact of how the city sorts applicants by legal form.

**Against the artefact reading, and the strongest single piece of counter-evidence to this note's own framing:** the Wmo day-activity ceiling is **fully committed, to the euro** **[D R18]**. That is not a filing artefact; it is a closed budget with two of these organisations inside it. Something real is zero-sum there.

**Both are true at once, in different domains.** The green domain has no competition because it has no market; the care domain has real competition because it has a capped one. **The competition between Eindhoven's green initiatives, insofar as it exists, is a competition between care providers** **[I]** — which is the least expected and most defensible finding here.

### 10.3 The corpus is the artefact

Every source here is promotional, institutional, journalistic or legal. An initiative's website describes its alliances; it does not describe its rivalries. A grant application is not public; a refusal is not published; a disagreement at a Groen Café leaves no trace. The absence of documented conflict is therefore **expected** and carries almost no information **[I]**. The conflicts that did surface are visible only because they are legal publications (a Didam notice, a DAEB decision, a subsidy register) or a published newsletter — **the tip of whatever is there**.

### 10.4 Tensions inside the evidence

- **"No food forest" versus a food forest.** R9 states the city has none; R8 reports one in het Henri Dunantpark. Both are documented statements; neither checked against a third source; Dunantpark's current existence is **[U]** beyond R8.
- **"Toegankelijk voor iedereen" versus a contract.** The estate opened publicly, and the same decision package forbids large-scale events, sports fields and campsites until at least 2032 **[D R5]**. Access and use are different things; "for everyone" describes the first.
- **A DAEB for "collectieve activiteiten van inwoners" that no resident body decides on.** The designation covers "collectieve, laagdrempelige maatschappelijke activiteiten" and was made with "geen besluitvorming door de raad vereist" **[D R20]**. The collective activities are named in the instrument; the collectivity is not a party to it.
- **150 volunteers and "the contact with other participants proves difficult for many".** Wasven reports the deepest volunteer base in the corpus **[D R21]** and, per the delegated line in #20, that contact among care participants is hard and volunteer policy was "nog niet van de grond gekomen" **(#20, delegated, not independently verified here)**. A large volunteer body and a community are not the same thing **[I]**.
- **A funded volunteer group is not a community.** The Wielewachters grow inside a branding line **[D R5]**. A volunteer body created as part of a *verfraaiing* budget is a different object from one formed to keep a place alive; whether the people in it experience the difference is **[U]**.
- **The closure was not a competition loss.** Its ground was private and was sold **[D R8; P]**. Framing it as outcompeted by the estate would be an invention; the defensible reading is that no route existed for it to be in the competition at all **[D R11, I]**.

### 10.5 Where this note qualifies the merged research

- **BRIEF §1.3** ("almost nothing reciprocal is documented: co-presence, not exchange") — **extended, not contradicted**: the co-presence has a consistent shape (one host, many guests), and that shape is *why* reciprocity does not develop: a host/guest relation transfers no obligation.
- **BRIEF §1.3** ("a portfolio difference, not a refusal") — **sharpened**: the portfolio difference has a doctrine (Didam continuity and anti-fragmentation, R22/R4), a legal instrument (a 10-year DAEB, R20), and an explicit clause excluding garden use (R11 art. 11(1)(b)). It is a portfolio difference **with a rulebook**.
- **#1 (network) does not have the Wasven–Trefpunt edge.** Its edge list records Trefpunt's relations to Wasven only as co-programming (eight Groen Cafés, §2 row 6) and does not record a **membership**. Wasven states it plainly: "Bij Trefpunt Groen Eindhoven zijn ruim honderd groepen aangesloten, waaronder het Wasven. Al sinds de oprichting in 2002 werken we samen om de kwaliteit van de leefomgeving te verbeteren" **[D R19]** — `https://wasven.nl/partners/trefpunt-groen-eindhoven/`. That is a standing affiliation, not an event, and it should be added to #1's edge list (type: membership; source: the member, not the body; date: continuous since 2002). The producer is Wasven, so it is one witness; Trefpunt's own report gives no member list, so the claim is **[C]** on both sides and uncorroborated.
- **#1 (network)**, which recorded Trefpunt's map as six extractable nodes — **corrected**: the map is a rendering; Trefpunt claims "ruim honderd groepen" **[C R19]**, and Wasven is a member **[D R19]**. The network is larger and more formal than the map suggests, and its centre is an **advocacy body with one municipal contract**, not a hub of mutual aid.
- **#1's finding that the food forest was never in Trefpunt's *achterban*** — **weakened to [U]**. The evidence was the absence from a rendered map (now known to be a rendering) and the absence of a member list in the annual report. Neither establishes absence. What stands is the narrower, verifiable negative: **the food forest appears nowhere in the 2025 municipal subsidy publication, and no retrieved source places it in the membership [D R18/U]**.
- **#27 (fragmentation)**: Wasven's presence and the gardens' absence on the 193-name partner list — **independently verified here [D R17]**, same caveat.
- **#27's "the plausible mechanism is earlier — small initiatives do not apply"** — **confirmed in a second domain, with numbers**: an unexhausted volunteer pot with a year-round €15,000 door and no green applicant **[D R18]**.

### 10.6 What the concepts named in the brief can and cannot carry

The issue lists five concepts as "useful starting concepts or literatures, not predetermined explanations". A bounded delegated line (line B §1–7) retrieved six documents against them and could not obtain a seventh. The outcome is a discipline, not a warrant; one of the five is unusable on the evidence retrieved.

| Concept | What was retrieved, at what level | What it licenses here | What it does **not** license | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Coopetition** | A review of 2,104 abstracts reporting **no effect sizes**; one qualitative study of 102 meetings in one US nonprofit | That competing with a partner for "funding, volunteers, and legitimacy" is an ordinary, recognised feature of the non-profit field — the resource set named almost exactly as this note names it **[D]** | That cooperating with a competitor is *stable*: the same review argues "stability in relationships can be a risk rather than a solution" **[D]**. Nor that asymmetry makes coopetition more fragile — **[U]** in everything retrieved | Framing and hypothesis-generator only |
| **Organisational ecology / niche theory** | A formal reconstruction of resource partitioning quoting Carroll & Hannan secondhand; primary texts **not obtained [U]** | Two precise terms: competition as *niche overlap* (tapping the same resource), and *realized* vs *fundamental* niche **[D]** | Any prediction that unequal organisations in a scarce niche specialise into complementarity. The theory assumes inert organisations, needs a population and a concentration statistic undefined at n = 7, and predicts competition where generalists have not vacated the periphery **[D/I]** | Definition and falsification prompt; **not** prediction |
| **Resource dependence** | A review (abstract only); one US merger study, 1,857 deals, whose authors call the theory "an appealing general metaphor, rather than a foundation for testable empirical research" **[D]** | The single best falsifiable expectation here: under power imbalance the dependent party is "willing, but not able to take action to reduce constraint" **[D]** — testable on the landless node | That the theory was tested here; any measurement of dependence; any reading of a strategy as an interest | One explicit analogy with a stated disconfirming observation |

**The one place where the literature cuts against this note's framing, recorded as such.** Resource partitioning predicts *competition*, not complementarity, precisely where the large generalists have not vacated the periphery. In Eindhoven they have not: the municipal estate and the care estate both run public-facing, volunteer-involving, nature-and-wellbeing programming **[D R6, R13]** — which is where the small gardens live **(#20)**, and where the food forest lived. On the theory's own logic that is niche overlap, not a division of labour. This note therefore reports complementarity as something **observed in five specific undertakings plus one shared adversarial interest** (§5), not as something the field's structure tends to produce.

## 13. Implications for the three pillars

**Pillar 1 — De Wielewaal as central green motor.** The evidence supports the *capital* and the *access*, and not the *motor*. The estate is a municipal portfolio with a national operator, €9.8 m and €600 k a year **[D R5, R6]**; its volunteer group is a **branding** item **[D R5]**; its contract forbids large-scale events until at least April 2032 **[D R5]**; its published partner set contains no Eindhoven green initiative **[D R6]**. A motor needs a transmission, and the only documented link from the estate to the rest of the green field is **Trefpunt's advisory relationship** **[D R1]**. Treating the estate as the ecosystem's centre would make the ecosystem a municipal portfolio with a public programme — a real thing, but not a network.

**Pillar 2 — connect existing initiatives into a city-wide ecosystem.** The record shows **four** working connective forms, all hosted or institutional: **a stage** (Groen Cafés), **a venue** (Wasven's erf), **a mandate** (Trefpunt's planning advice), and **a membership** (~100 groups, Wasven included) **[D R1, R7, R19]**. It shows no shared infrastructure, no shared obligation, no reciprocity of work, and — the newly measured fact — **no competitive market in green money to coordinate** **[D R18]**. Connecting on this evidence means one of two things: (a) use the existing hosts deliberately — a slot, a stall, a theme, a membership — which is available now and costs almost nothing; or (b) change what a party must *be* in order to hold ground, which is a municipal decision no initiative can make. The evidence supports (a) as available and supports no claim that (a) produces an ecosystem. It also warns that the one place where real consolidation would be zero-sum is the **care** budget, not the green one **[D R18]**.

**Pillar 3 — nature closer to everyday life.** Here competition is least contested and complementarity most real: audiences are not scarce, spaces are, and the documented overlaps (estate visitor centre, care farm, gardens, a buurttuin's daily unpurposed use) are additive in experience even where they overlap in offer **[D R6, R13, R16; #20]**. The binding constraint is the same as everywhere in this note: the places that host everyday nature are hosted by someone, and the host's continuity is the practice's continuity **(#20)**.

**Community as foundation.** On this evidence "the community" is three different things in three registers: an **audience** (list, visitors, market public), a **constituency** (Trefpunt's *achterban*, which is organisations), and a **workforce** (volunteers — 150 of them inside Wasven, ~30 at the food forest). The food forest had the first, never had the second, and lost the third with its ground **[D R9, R2, R8, R21]**. A pillar that says "community is the foundation" is therefore ambiguous in a way that matters: it can be satisfied by an audience, and an audience is the cheapest thing in this system to hold.

## 15. Method, limits, delegation and verification

**Method.** Desk research against public sources only, retrieved 26 November 2026 through signed `source_read` requests. No stakeholder contacted; no form, login, closed group or social platform entered; nothing implemented; nobody brokered.
