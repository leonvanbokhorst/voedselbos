# Contrasts and counterexamples: practices that changed, stalled or were formalised

**Issues:** #20 (what existing Eindhoven green practices teach about the conditions under which people come to enjoy caring for their environment together).
**Line of work:** the issue's explicit demand for counterexamples — practices that struggled, changed or stopped, and informal or less publicly visible ones — so that success narratives can be tested.
**Status:** desk research, public sources only, all retrievals 26 November 2026. No stakeholder contact, no forms, no logins, no closed groups. No subagents.
**Labels:** **[D]** documented in a retrieved source · **[C]** claimed by a single source · **[I]** inference of mine · **[U]** not established in the sources searched · **[R]** already established in this repository (cited, not re-researched).

**Headline caveat, stated up front.** Of the five "changed or stopped" candidates in the brief, **only Voedselbos Eindhoven is documented as having stopped**. For the other four, the "changed/stopped" framing is *not* supported by any source I retrieved; in two cases the evidence points the other way (Struikroven grew and was formalised; Buurttuin vol Kleur is still running as a programme). This is itself the most useful result of the line: the appeal of a "failed initiative" story is easy to generate from a funding record and hard to substantiate.

The volunteers are counted as line items in someone else's hours. That asymmetry is a finding in its own right for #20 — and it is a limit on any claim of the form "Eindhoven has N initiatives".

## 2. Contrast profiles

### 2.1 Struikroven — an initiative that changed form until it stopped being an initiative

**What it is now [D].** "Struikroven redt het bestaande groen uit voor-en achtertuinen en geven het een nieuwe bestemming in een bestaande omgeving… in opdracht van woningcorporaties, projectontwikkelaars of gemeenten" — with a "Roverstuin" as the holding site where lifted plants are heeled in and later "op een sociale manier gedistribueerd" ([TGE netwerk page](https://www.trefpuntgroeneindhoven.nl/netwerk/struikroven), undated, retrieved 26-11-2026).

**The documented change [D].** TGE: "Struikroven is begonnen als buurtinitiatief in Eindhoven, maar is inmiddels een snelgroeiende, landelijke stichting." The municipality puts the same trajectory in institutional terms, dated **25 January 2021**: "Struikroven begon ooit als bewonersinitiatief en is nu uitgegroeid tot een gewoonte binnen het Duurzaamheidspact… Het Duurzaamheidspact … heeft afgesproken dat bij elk sloop- of renovatieproject eerst wordt gestruikroofd" ([eindhovenduurzaam.nl](https://www.eindhovenduurzaam.nl/nieuws/bij-renovatie-en-sloop-altijd-struikroven), 25-01-2021, retrieved 26-11-2026). The Duurzaamheidspact's parties are named there: corporaties 'thuis, Sint Trudo, Woonbedrijf en Wooninc., huurdersvertegenwoordiging PEK, and the municipality.

**Why this is a contrast, not a success story.** Three shifts are documented in one practice:
1. **Actor shift** — residents lifting plants from their own street → a national foundation working "in opdracht van" housing corporations, developers and municipalities.
2. **Norm shift** — voluntary neighbourliness → a clause in a covenant that makes struikroven the *default* at every demolition and renovation project.
3. **Voice shift** — the source that now describes the practice publicly is the municipality and the intermediary, not the residents. TGE's own gloss is the tell: "Zij geloven dat bewoners 'caretakers' van de natuur worden met Struikroven" and "Met Struikroven wordt de gemeenschapszin in wijken verbeterd, wat groen verbindt!" **[C]** — a claim about residents' inner disposition, asserted by an intermediary, with no resident quoted.

**Whose voice is audible:** the municipality's and TGE's. The sessions are described in the second person ("Tijdens een struikroofsessie ga je samen met een ervaren struikrover en een groenexpert langs bij een pand") **[D]** — an invitation, not testimony. **No participant is quoted in any source read** [D for the absence].

**What cannot be established [U]:** whether residents still initiate sessions or now mostly attend ones organised for them; how many people take part per year; what happened to the original Eindhoven group as an organisation; whether "landelijk" means offices, staff or a website. **The change is documented; its cost or benefit for participants is not.**

**Counter-inference, stated:** scaling and covenanting a practice is also how a volunteer practice survives its founders. Nothing in the record says the original residents were displaced; the shift may be pure gain. The honest formulation is that the practice **changed hands and changed genre**, and that the sources which describe it now are not the sources that would notice a loss.

### 2.2 Voedselbosje bij De Splinter — the initiative that has not (yet) arrived

**What is documented [D]** (TGE netwerk page, undated, retrieved 26-11-2026): WIJ Eindhoven's neighbourhood-connection arm *Buurt in Bloei* met two neighbours, "Kim en Eloise", who "hadden al langer het plan om een buurtmoestuin op te zetten"; the plan is for "een voedselbosje vol eetbare planten en bomen" serving as "een soort buurtmoestuin" opposite speelpark De Splinter.

**The grind, in the page's own words [D]:**
> "De afgelopen twee jaar zijn er vele vergaderingen geweest. Het voedselbos zal gepland worden op gemeentegrond wat zo zijn drempels met zich meebracht. Nu is er groen licht en kan er geld bij elkaar worden verzameld om het voedselbos aan te leggen."
> *(Gloss: the past two years there have been many meetings. The food forest will be planted on municipal ground, which brought its own thresholds. Now there is green light and money can be raised to lay it out.)*

And: "er is namelijk nog ruimte voor vrijwilligers of donaties" **[D]**.

**Why this is the sharpest counterexample in the set.** The bottleneck is not enthusiasm, not soil, not knowledge, not even land — the land is municipal — but **the sequence**: two years of meetings, then permission, then fundraising, then planting. Compare Voedselbos Eindhoven, which had land, plants, thirty volunteers and twelve years of practice, and lost the site because the land was private **[R]**. The two cases fail at opposite ends: one had access without permission, the other permission without money.

**Whose voice is audible:** TGE's, describing two named neighbours in the third person. The two initiators are **named** (Kim, Eloise) but **not quoted** [D]. A WIJ Eindhoven staff member's e-mail address is given as the contact point [D] — the institutional midwife is the public face, not the neighbours.

**What cannot be established [U]:** the page is **undated**, so "two years ago" cannot be converted into a calendar date; whether funding was raised; whether the food forestje was planted; whether Kim and Eloise are still involved. The 2026 press that names the Henri Dunantpark food forest as the city's remaining case does not mention De Splinter **[C]** ([eindhoven.nieuws.nl](https://eindhoven.nieuws.nl/gemeente/eindhoven-ontbreekt-op-landelijke-voedselbossendag-na-plotselinge-sluiting-voedselbos), 25-08-2026, as recorded in `research/continuity/sources.md`). **Absence from one article is not evidence of failure.**

### 2.3 Buurttuin vol Kleur — the "one-season grant" that turns out to be a running programme

The brief's candidate framing was "one-season Postcode Loterij Fonds grant, Groenewoudseweg". **The funding record says exactly that and no more [D]:**

> "Buurttuin 'Groenewoud' in Eindhoven wordt in gebruik genomen door bewoners en kinderen via de GroenKLUP. Er worden workshops georganiseerd om te leren werken met natuurlijke verfplanten. In de tuin groeien inheemse planten waarmee textiel op ecologische wijze gekleurd wordt."
> Bedrag **€ 5.000** · Periode **Voorjaar 2026** · Locatie **Groenewoudseweg, 5621BJ EINDHOVEN** · Organisatie **Stichting Kansen voor Kinderen** · Categorie Buurtprojecten
> ([postcodeloterijfonds.nl/portfolio/buurttuin-vol-kleur](https://www.postcodeloterijfonds.nl/portfolio/buurttuin-vol-kleur/), retrieved 26-11-2026; sha256 `890fc28f…`)

**A grant is not a garden, and a lapsed grant is not a stopped garden — here the garden is documented as running [D].** Stichting Kansen voor Kinderen's own site describes the GroenKLUP as a weekly activity in "de buurttuin 'Het Groenewoud'": "Samen zaaien en oogsten ze nieuwe planten… en creëren ze uiteindelijk een bloeiende moes- en kruidentuin"; children "worden ondersteund door ervaren begeleiders"; the location is "Buurttuin 'Het Groene Woud' … naast de sportvelden aan de Groenewoudseweg", with children gathering at KLUPhuis 70, Edisonstraat ([kansenvoorkinderen.nl/groenklup](https://kansenvoorkinderen.nl/groenklup/), undated, retrieved 26-11-2026; sha256 `338dbb98…`). A city volunteering event lists a working session there: "Samen met Kansen voor kinderen maken we de buurttuin nog mooier… We doen dit samen met tuinarchitect Marije van Studio van der Park en de groenKLUP", at "de buurttuin aan de Groenewoudseweg, ter hoogte van de Ampèrestraat" ([groenewijkweek.nl](https://groenewijkweek.nl/programma_item/meehelpen-in-de-buurttuin/), dated "zo 13 okt", **year not stated on the page**; 13 October fell on a Sunday in 2024, not 2025 [I]; sha256 `46fdf01e…`).

**So the real contrast is a different one — about form, not failure.** This is not a residents' garden that a grant kept alive for a season. It is **a youth-work organisation's activity in a public-space garden**, with paid "ervaren begeleiders", a commissioned tuinarchitect, a school-age programme and a philanthropic grant as one income line among others. The "bewoners" in the funder's description are the *neighbourhood*, addressed as beneficiaries and invited in ("Als ouder ben je ook van harte welkom om je eigen groene vingers uit te leven, de buurttuin is toegankelijk voor de hele buurt!" **[D]**), not as the practice's authors.

**Whose voice is audible:** the funder's (portfolio prose), the youth-work organisation's (programme prose). **No child, parent or volunteer is quoted** [D for the absence]. The one nearby sign of organisational strain is a **blocked** page: `eindhovendoet.nl/vacatures/coordinator-groenklup` returned **403, cf-ray `a60b82c78915655a-AMS`** (retrieved 26-11-2026). A URL slug is not a posting — **whether a paid coordinator is being recruited, and why, is [U]**. That is a lead, not evidence.

**What cannot be established [U]:** when the buurttuin started; what happens to it after "Voorjaar 2026"; how many residents use it; whether the € 5.000 was for materials, staff or workshops; whether the natural-dye workshop strand survives.

### 2.4 Groen Bennekel — informal, active, and almost invisible in public

**Documented [D]:** "In de wijk Bennekel zet een groep vrijwilligers zich in voor een groene, schone en gezonde wijk"; Groen Bennekel is "een werkgroep van het Buurtplatform Bennekel Belang"; it works "in samenwerking met de gemeente en Woonbedrijf" on "geveltuintjes, moestuinen en bloembakken"; it was started "omdat Bennekel een groenarme wijk is"; one volunteer, Anna Chang, "heeft niet alleen haar eigen tuin omgebouwd tot een ware moestuin maar plant en onderhoud ook groen op andere plekken in de straat en helpt buren bij het vergroenen van de tuin"; the buurttuin *De Buurtwerf*, next to wijkcentrum De Dommel, "is nog volop in ontwikkeling" ([TGE netwerk page](https://www.trefpuntgroeneindhoven.nl/netwerk/groen-bennekel), undated, retrieved 26-11-2026; sha256 `210ce6fc…`).

**The visibility contrast [D].** The group's own website, `groenbennekel.nl`, is a Google Sites shell: the retrieval returned 420 characters of navigation, "Follow us on Facebook and Instagram!", and a cookie notice — **no activities, no dates, no contact** (retrieved 26-11-2026; sha256 `8a6e83b0…`). TGE's page anyway redirects the reader to "de Facebookpagina GroenBennekel of de website van GroenBennekel" **[D]** — i.e. the durable public record of an active neighbourhood group lives on a platform this research cannot and does not enter. **Its current level of activity is [U]; its existence as an organised group is [D].**

Also visible in TGE's own reporting: an "Actiedag 'Vele groene Handen' in de Bennekel" photo caption in the 2024 jaarverslag **[D]** (p. 15) — evidence of a joint action day, no numbers.

## 3. Wasven: a different institutional form

### 3.1 Form and legal shape [D]
Three foundations, all ANBI, with a **personele unie** board of five (Alfred van Kempen chair, Eva van Roosmalen secretary, Felix Pessers treasurer, Robert van Dijck, Geert van Seeters) and a two-person Raad van toezicht: Stichting Groendomein Wasven (RSIN 814421635, KvK 17176851), Stichting Wasvenboerderij (RSIN 817342539, KvK 17198973), Stichting Wasvenzorg (RSIN 854641051, KvK 62086839) ([wasven.nl/ondernemen/besturen](https://wasven.nl/ondernemen/besturen/), retrieved 26-11-2026; sha256 `5972aa30…`). "Het Wasven is organisatorisch en financieel zelfstandig. De drie stichtingen vormen tezamen een fiscale eenheid voor de omzetbelasting" ([verantwoording](https://wasven.nl/verantwoording/); sha256 `6f2fda0b…`). Quality of care is anchored via the Raad van toezicht under the Governancecode Zorg and the "Kwaliteit laat je zien" keurmerk; operations carry EKO and Green Key **[D]**.

**Land [D]:** the municipality is giving ground in the Warande in use to Stichting Groendomein Wasven, "gericht op ecologisch natuurbeheer, biologische landbouw (agroforestry) en maatschappelijke doeleinden zoals dagbesteding, re-integratie en vrijwilligerswerk" — a **single-candidate** procedure, motivated on continuity: "Deze functies zijn nauw verbonden met het sociale en ecologische karakter van het Groendomein en kunnen niet zonder risico's van verstoring of discontinuïteit aan een andere partij worden overgedragen" ([Gemeenteblad 2026 nr. 14748](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2026-14748.pdf), 15-01-2026, retrieved 26-11-2026, sha256 `72714337…`). A reported additional municipal guarantee of € 0,4 m (2023) is a **snippet only** in this repository and is treated as a lead, not a finding [C] (`research/continuity/sources.md` §1.3).

### 3.2 Scale and staffing [D]
- **Day-activity participants:** 44 at the start of 2024, 4 joined, 5 left, **45 at the end of 2024**; day-parts up 2,3 %; "Een deelnemer is gemiddeld 4 tot 5 dagdelen op het Wasven"; funding via **WMO, WLZ and PGB**; care intensity **VG 2 t/m 7**; day activities in the tuinderij expanded from 3 to 5 day-parts a week ([Kwaliteitsjaarverslag Wasvenzorg 2024](https://wasven.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Jaarverslag-Kwaliteitszorg-Wasven-2024.pdf), dated 23-02-2025, retrieved 26-11-2026, sha256 `55a15cd9…`, §4.1).
- **Staffing model:** teams of "vakmensen, vrijwilligers en deelnemers", each under a **teamleider**, across horeca, retail, zorg, groen and gemeenschap, plus administration, HR and facilities ([Teamwerk](https://wasven.nl/werken/organisatie/); sha256 `985357ce…`). Stagiairs in 2024: two HBO Social Work students and six secondary-school maatschappelijke stages **[D]** (Kwaliteitsjaarverslag §4.4).
- **Volunteers:** organised in **ten werkgroepen** — Communicatie, Educatie, Natuurbeheer, Natuurbeleving, Imkerij, Tuinderij, Winkel, Bakkerij, Voedselbeleving, Branderij, plus Vervoer and "Maak het mee" — "ondersteund en begeleid door teamleiders", on fixed days, rosters or project basis ([Vrijwilligerswerk](https://wasven.nl/werken/vrijwilligerswerk/); sha256 `a6c8e510…`). **No total headcount of volunteers is published in any source read** [U].
- **Recruitment** runs through Eindhoven Doet, NL Doet in March and the Landelijke Natuurwerkdag in November **[D]**.

### 3.3 Who the participants publicly are, and what the organisation says about them
The target group is specified in clinical and funding terms, not in aspirational ones: "Onze dagbesteding is geschikt voor mensen die individuele ondersteuning nodig hebben in groepsverband. Voor mensen met een verstandelijke beperking, autisme, ADHD, angststoornis, hechtingsproblematiek, niet aangeboren hersenletsel (NAH) of lichamelijke beperking" — with "Een positieve motivatie is een belangrijke randvoorwaarde" ([doelgroepen](https://wasven.nl/zorg/dagbesteding/doelgroepen/); sha256 `6fe3e415…`). "Maak het mee" extends nature experience to "kwetsbare doelgroepen… mensen met een verstandelijke beperking, psychiatrische achtergrond en voor mantelzorgers" **[D]** (Vrijwilligerswerk page).

### 3.4 Whose voice is audible — and what is documented against interest
- **Two volunteer quotations exist, both anonymous and curated:** a *stoker* ("Het geeft voldoening om elke keer weer een geslaagd product te maken… En gelukzalige vermoeidheid achteraf") and a *chauffeur* ("Als chauffeur van het Wasven is de flexibiliteit super fijn…") **[D]** (Vrijwilligerswerk page). They are the organisation's own selection — evidence of what the organisation chooses to publish, not of what volunteers think.
- **Deelnemers are never quoted.** Their views appear only as third-person summary in the care quality report: "Deelnemers geven aan het lastig te vinden als taken niet duidelijk zijn of als zij verschillende instructies krijgen"; "Het contact met andere deelnemers blijkt voor veel deelnemers lastig, ze geven aan daarin veel begeleiding nodig te hebben"; satisfaction questionnaire response 55 %, done on paper with smileys at participants' request **[D]** (Kwaliteitsjaarverslag §6.5–6.6).
- **Documented against the promotional grain [D]:** "Het formaliseren van het vrijwilligersbeleid, als vervolg op het project Waardering, is nog niet van de grond gekomen. De teamleiders hebben hun handen vol aan de operationele taken en het aansturen van de teams" (§4.5); 27 recorded incidents among 18 participants and 20 behaviour reports concerning 2 participants (§7.1, §7.3); employee satisfaction survey response 30 % (§4.6); the two-yearly information meeting for participants and relatives "wordt als waardevol ervaren maar wordt relatief weinig bezocht" (§6.4).

**Reading [I].** The Wasven is the only practice in this set whose public record contains metrics *and* admissions of shortfall — because it is the only one that must report to a care regulator, a certifier and a municipality. Its durability is not evidence that togetherness is easier there. The one explicitly documented difficulty is precisely the thing #20 asks about: **contact between participants is hard and needs continuous professional guidance**, and the volunteer layer runs on teamleiders who are, by the organisation's own account, at capacity. Institutional form buys continuity and produces documentation; it does not by itself produce the enjoyment of caring together, and it may route the voice of participants through a professional reporting chain rather than through their own words.

## 4. What the contrasts suggest about the conditions for durability — **inference, clearly marked** [I]

1. **Sequence, not enthusiasm, decides whether a resident initiative arrives.** De Splinter has neighbours, a site, a partner and municipal consent, and after two years of meetings is still raising money; Voedselbos Eindhoven had people and plants and no tenure. A practice needs *land, permission and money at the same time* before a single planting, and the gap between those three is where initiatives wait.
2. **The durable forms in this set all have a payer with a non-garden motive.** Wasven runs on care funding (WMO/WLZ/PGB) and municipal ground with a continuity rationale; Buurttuin Het Groenewoud runs inside a youth-work organisation; Struikroven runs on corporate and municipal contracts. The volunteer-only, gift-and-grant forms (Voedselbos Eindhoven; the Postcode Loterij strand) are the fragile ones.
3. **Formalisation is how a resident practice survives — and how residents stop being its authors.** Struikroven's trajectory (neighbourliness → covenant default → national stichting) is the clearest documented case; the sources describing it afterwards are the municipality and the intermediary, and no participant speaks in them.
4. **Public visibility is inversely related to informality.** Groen Bennekel is active and its own website is empty; its real record is on Facebook. Any enumeration built from searchable public pages will systematically miss exactly the informal practices #20 asks for.

**Counter-inference, which must be carried alongside all four.** (a) Longevity is not the only good: a covenant that makes struikroven routine may deliver more plants and more neighbours per year than any single garden, and calling that "losing authorship" is a value judgement, not a finding. (b) Documented admissions are a function of reporting obligations, so Wasven looks more troubled than an unaccountable garden of the same quality — the comparison is biased in the *opposite* direction from the promotional sources. (c) Nothing retrieved measures enjoyment, satisfaction or care at the resident initiatives; "conditions for durability" is a survival question, and **survival is not the same as people coming to enjoy caring together** — a point the issue itself insists on. (d) The four inferences rest on six to eight cases selected by the availability of documents, not by any sampling logic.
