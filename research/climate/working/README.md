**Mark conventions.** The repository uses **[D]** documented, **[C]** claimed, **[I]** inferred, **[U]** not established, **[P]** commissioner-provided. The delegated files use the same marks; each file's header states its own read-depth conventions.

**Delegation record.** Four bounded researchers on disjoint files and disjoint source families, each told to retrieve rather than recall, record read depth and hashes, hunt unflattering evidence and null findings, return "not established" rather than fill a gap, not read sibling outputs, and not spawn further agents. The Lead re-read nine sources in full or in part after the lines returned (listed in `../note.md` §6 and `../sources.md`), found one correction to the issue brief (the 200 m² figure), one qualified child claim (lead contamination), and one reused-claim discrepancy in the sibling evidence base (`EHV-C-0428`), and labelled everything it did not verify.

**What is not here.** No stakeholder was contacted; no login, paywall, CAPTCHA or Cloudflare challenge was bypassed; no personal or participant data was collected; nothing here allocates a site or proposes a parcel.
