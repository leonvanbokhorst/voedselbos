# Working file — delegated lines (not evidence in themselves)

Two subagents were used for genuinely independent lines of investigation. This file records the briefs and what was carried. **Nothing here is evidence**; every claim used in [`../note.md`](../note.md) points at a source in [`../sources.md`](../sources.md), and the ones I did not re-retrieve myself are marked as child-reported.

## Line 1 — civic technology, maintenance, automation bias

**Brief:** establish (1) maintenance/decay of civic tech and community platforms; (2) maintenance-and-repair studies as a scholarly frame; (3) volunteer/open-source governance sustainability and key-person risk; (4) digital divide and channel proliferation; (5) automation bias experiments; (6) "AI as infrastructure", including non-vendor evaluations of Dutch↔English MT and of speech recognition; (7) any documented instance of AI/software in volunteer coordination or community-garden management. Rules: `source_read` only, verbatim quotes, dates, no invention, "not established" permitted, no further spawning.

**What it returned that I kept:** the Hamm et al. and Rehak civic-tech-discontinuation pair; Ramakrishnan, O'Reilly & Budds on maintenance/decay/repair; Lyell et al. on automation bias (with figures I corrected against the paper); Coelho & Valente on OSS abandonment; the uHelp mutual-aid prototype; the INNOVADE "7.8% inactive" figure (kept only to decline to use it as a rate); the Pijnacker-Nootdorp audit for "kanaalverleiding".

**What I discarded or downgraded:** the "56.9% more commission errors" figure (not in the paper); the Dutch ASR result reported without the medium-model comparison; the INNOVADE claim about channel parity, which its own brief asserts rather than measures; and several citations it could not retrieve (Parasuraman & Riley 1997, Skitka et al. 1999, Russell & Vinsel 2018, Jackson 2014), which are therefore not used at all rather than cited from memory.

## Line 2 — task-level technical and regulatory evidence

**Brief:** establish (1) independent MT evaluations with numbers, Dutch↔English included; (2) plain-language simplification reliability; (3) speech transcription accuracy on Dutch and on spontaneous/code-switched audio; (4) retrieval-grounded answering reliability; (5) acting agents' failure modes and accountability, from primary standards/regulator text; (6) Dutch/EU data-protection material on chatbots, LLMs and small organisations; (7) function creep in community settings; (8) measured administrative burden for small nonprofits. Same rules.

**What it returned that I kept:** the AP/ACM joint statement on chatbots and the AP companion-app investigation (both re-retrieved by me and quoted verbatim); the AP generative-AI guidance and EDPB Opinion 28/2024 (referenced, not quoted); the UK NCSC prompt-injection post (re-retrieved); the MaTIAS machine-translation study (re-retrieved in depth); the TU/e–TNO Dutch ASR paper (re-retrieved, with the medium-model comparison added); RAGTruth, AgentDojo and Koenecke et al. (carried as child-reported, supporting only); the NOV/Movisie 2015 survey of Dutch volunteer organisations (re-retrieved).

**What I discarded or downgraded:** the Radunović et al. BNAIC 2026 pension-domain MT paper — relevant but formal-register Dutch-to-English, and its own English result has a confidence interval crossing zero, so it would have been used to make a point it does not clearly make; the EU AI Act article numbers, which I did not retrieve in the original and therefore do not quote (the note refers only to "the AI Act's deployer duties" and marks the application **[U]**); and a claimed "100% hallucination-free" result in one retrieved RCT, which I judged an implausible self-assessment and did not use.

## Corrections made to delegated material before use

Both corrections are recorded in [`../note.md`](../note.md) §11.1 and [`../sources.md`](../sources.md) §4. The general rule applied: a child's finding was used only where I had either re-retrieved the source myself, or the claim was supporting rather than pivotal and marked as child-reported.
