## 4. Verification record

**Pivotal claims I re-retrieved and checked against the original, with what changed:**

- **CEP reporting hours.** The first URL I tried (`cep.org/reimagining-reporting-part-1-insights-from-the-field/`) returned **404**; the canonical blog URL was found and retrieved. The sentence "dropping from eight hours for a single funder's requirements to six hours" is verbatim, and so is the caveat that the sample of 96 "is not fully representative of the field". The delegated report had the number right; the note adds the caveat.
- **Lyell et al. automation bias.** The delegated report's "56.9% more commission errors" is not in the paper. The retrieved figures are commission-error rates of **65.8%, 53.5% and 51.7%** across the three conditions, and omission-error increases of **33.3%, 24.5% and 26.7%**. The note uses the retrieved figures. The delegated report's "interruptions did not affect errors" is confirmed verbatim.
- **Dutch ASR.** The delegated report's 0.691 WER for the large model is correct and I verified it in Table 2 — but its framing omitted that the *medium* model scores 0.266 on the same data, i.e. the headline is a model-size anomaly as much as a Dutch-language finding. The note states both.
- **MaTIAS.** Verified the XSTS results table (Tigrinya 2.73, Somali 2.96, "only 29% of Tigrinya messages and 40% of Somali messages received an averaged shifted score higher than 3"), the one-way design, the interpreter condition, and the two Dutch compound failures verbatim.
- **Macken's "Dutch is harder"** claim: verified as a ChrF statement about source language ("translations from English consistently achieving higher ChrF scores compared to those from French or Dutch") and as an XSTS statement ("For nine out of the 14 languages, the mean values are slightly higher for translations from French than for translations from Dutch"). The note reports it as such, not as a general claim about Dutch.
- **AP/ACM and AP companion-app findings.** Both quoted verbatim from the regulators' own pages, including the exact wording of the "always offer a human employee" condition and the evasive-answer finding.
- **Trefpunt Groen hours — corrected in this revision.** The *Urenbesteding 2025* is a **pie chart, not a table**. I re-fetched the PDF directly and confirmed the bytes are identical to the `source_read` retrieval (sha256 begins `7ee352566e828c25`, matching [S20]), then extracted word coordinates with `pdftotext -bbox` (evidence: [`working/urenbesteding-coordinates.md`](working/urenbesteding-coordinates.md)). That was an over-assertion of exactly the kind this repository's review tests for, and it has been removed rather than qualified.
