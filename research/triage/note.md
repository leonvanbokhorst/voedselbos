# Which open issues still bear on the main question — a triage, not a research report

**Lead triage, 26 November 2026.** This note does not research any of the questions it lists. It records the judgement a PI or researcher needs before choosing one: after the 26 November 2026 wave, which open issues still bear on [`QUESTION.md`](../../QUESTION.md), what merged research already covers, and what a research pass would be authorised to do. It marks its own claims **[D]** documented in a retrieved source, **[I]** inference of this note, **[U]** not established.

## 1. The problem this triage answers

Eight issues were open when this pass began: #4, #5, #6, #9, #10, #12, #14 and #19. The repository has meanwhile grown to thirteen research notes, a synthesis brief and two independent reviews. That creates two failure modes a triage should prevent: **re-researching a question that a merged note has already answered**, and **leaving a question open that nothing is going to answer** because it was in fact researched and the issue was simply never closed.

The second one is not hypothetical. **Issue #19 is answered and merged, and still open** (§4).

## 2. The criterion used for "still relevant"

An open issue is treated as still relevant only if all three hold:

1. **It bears on the main question as now framed.** `QUESTION.md`'s focus clarification (Voedselbos Eindhoven closed July 2026; Voedselbos 2.0 is a pre-institutional practice organised around visibility of the movement; De Wielewaal is context, not the centre) is part of the brief, so a question that only worked while a central green motor was assumed is not automatically still relevant.
2. **Merged research does not already answer it.** Where a note establishes most of the answer, the remaining gap is named; where the gap is small, the honest action is a comment on the issue, not a new report.
3. **It is answerable under the working constraints** in `QUESTION.md`: public sources, no stakeholder contact, no fieldwork, no implementation. A question whose answer exists only in interviews is a question for a later, consented phase — not a desk-research task.

## 4. Issue #19 is answered and open — the one administrative correction

- **The research exists and is merged.** PR [#21](https://github.com/leonvanbokhorst/voedselbos/pull/21) was merged on 26 November 2026 at 20:01 with the title "Research: what makes shared care meaningful — and how community grows through it (#19)", and its primary output is [`research/meaning/note.md`](../meaning/note.md) **[D]**.
- **The note answers the issue's own scope.** It covers the four things the issue asked for — meaning and motivation, the relational processes, uncertainty and rival interpretations, and conversation prompts — and it states the limit the issue anticipated: no verbatim participant voice exists in the public record **[D]**.
- **The issue remains open.** It appears in `gh issue list --state open` with no comments and no labels **[D]**, while its siblings from the same wave (#20, #22, #25, #27) were closed. The discrepancy looks procedural rather than substantive **[I]**.
- **Recommendation:** close #19 with a pointer to PR #21 and [`research/meaning/note.md`](../meaning/note.md). The only thing that would justify reopening it is a consented conversation phase, which the issue's own exclusions defer.

**What the seven passes changed, read together.** Three things recur across notes that were researched independently:

1. **The binding constraint is not goodwill but tenure, mandate and money — and the money is small and already allocated.** #4 finds the land instruments decided before application; #5 finds every durable exchange has a payer; #6 finds the ecological constraint already written into zoning; #14 finds the previous connective layer died without a legal person. The repository's earlier finding that "structure is necessary and insufficient" survives, with the structural part now documented in more detail.
2. **The figure the ecosystem is most likely to quote is the weakest one.** #9 shows the participation numbers are staff codings; #10 shows the city's own health gain has no derivation; #6 shows the capacity discourse has no threshold. Each pass independently recommends stating the definition before the number.
3. **Nobody in the record speaks for themselves.** The participant-silence finding that #19 established for the closed food forest is now the explicit limit of #5, #9 and #10 as well. Four independent passes hit the same wall, which strengthens it: it is a property of the public record, not of one research design.

**Corrections this wave raised against merged notes.** Three claims in already-merged notes did not survive re-retrieval. Two were verified at source by the Lead and corrected in place, with the original wording kept visible and a dated correction line: the Klusbus is an energy-poverty service, not coordination infrastructure ([`research/experiments/note.md`](../experiments/note.md) §1), and Wasven's volunteer rhythm is one Saturday cycle, not two ([`research/network/sources.md`](../network/sources.md) S4). The third — the sibling evidence base's "Woensel-West Verhalen is empty" — is outside this repository and is flagged in [`research/community/note.md`](../community/note.md) rather than edited. A fourth, raised from inside #4's own drafting, is a defect rather than a correction: a `sources.md` editing pass had deleted entries the note still cited, restored and recorded in that note's §15.

**What the wave did not do.** It did not update [`research/BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) or the README index. Seven new notes change enough of the synthesis — the land and money picture in §1.3–1.4, the "+2 gezonde jaren" in §4, the participation counts — that a separate synthesis pass is the honest next step rather than a paragraph appended here. It also did not close #19 (§4) and did not reconcile the unmerged `review/26-28-proportionality` branch.

**Updated in the same wave, after this note was filed.** That synthesis pass has since been done, on its own branch rather than by editing this note: [`research/synthesis/note.md`](../synthesis/note.md) reads all seven together, tests which of their agreements are independent, and names where they narrow or correct each other. [`research/BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) and the README index were revised in the same pull request. The two notes are complements, not versions of each other: this one triages what was worth researching, that one reads what came back.

**One limitation withdrawn after filing.** The #10 pass first reported its ecological half as thin because a delegated line had not returned. That line did return, and the pass verified it at source: the temperate food-forest scoping review records ten comparator studies and none on interaction with surrounding land use, half of all studies sit in a single food forest, and a full-text search of that review returns **zero** occurrences of *infiltration*, *hydrology*, *soil moisture* or *runoff* — so the water pathway has a documented absence of evidence rather than an assumed one **[D, verified by the Lead on the same PDF]**. What survives is sharper than the withdrawn claim: Dutch ecological protocols do exist (EU 2025/2360 descriptors, NDFF/FLORON, Sovon MUS and BMP-A, Bodemdierendagen, NMVB, i-Tree Canopy), but every one measures a grid square, an area of 10–250 ha, a postcode or a 30-site programme — **none measures a 0.1–1 ha garden**. See [`research/health-outcomes/note.md`](../health-outcomes/note.md) §4.4.
