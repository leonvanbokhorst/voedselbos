# Participation and the municipal interface — notes on issues #3 and #8

Scope: barriers to participation and volunteer exhaustion in Eindhoven's green initiatives (#3), and how the
municipality shapes what green community initiatives can do (#8). Public sources only; no stakeholders contacted.
Marks: **[D]** documented in a retrievable source; **[C]** claimed by a party (incl. the sibling evidence base, whose
claims are provisional); **[I]** inference of this note; **[N]** not established. All retrievals 26 November 2026;
URLs and dates in `sources.md`.

## 1. The case that anchors both questions

Voedselbos Eindhoven (Elburglaan 8, edge of landgoed De Wielewaal, Strijp) closed permanently. Its own site says
"Permanent gesloten sinds juli 2026" and carries a notice dated **28 July 2026**: closure "met onmiddellijke
ingang" after "meer dan 12 jaar", thanking "alle vrijwilligers die iedere vrijdag hebben meegebouwd" **[D]**. Press
adds the sequence: the plan was to stop after the summer on the same site, then in early August the gates shut at
once; the site is "particulier eigendom", which "maakte de toekomst van het initiatief kwetsbaar" **[D]**. The
proximate condition is therefore **tenure on private land**, not a municipal refusal. What the city did publicly is
narrower: end-June 2026 wethouder Rik Thijs spoke with residents, and the municipality said it would "meedenken" about
a new location; none has been announced **[D]**.

Two contrasts matter. A second food forest in **Henri Dunantpark** (Woensel-Noord) grew from residents in a public
park and was likewise absent from the national open-day map **[D]**. The adjacent estate: the municipality bought
**Landgoed De Wielewaal in February 2022** "met als doel het landgoed toegankelijk te maken voor iedereen", lifted a
2007 openstellingsverbod (running to 2032, €3.6 m penalty) in a package deal with the Erven Philips, and opened it on
27–28 September 2025, with a **€9.8 m** Lichtbos programme and **€600 k per year** structural management money; the
Omgevingsvisie counts **+145 ha** partly public green **[D]**. A twelve-year community food forest on private land at
that estate's edge closed while the public green beside it got a nine-figure opening programme. Not hypocrisy — a
**portfolio difference**: municipal money follows land the municipality owns, and initiative on land it does not own
has no instrument.

## 2. Issue #8 — enabler, gatekeeper, absent partner

**Policy stack, status verified.** The **Omgevingsvisie** was adopted 26 November 2024 (Gemeenteblad 2024, 525496);
its first verstedelijkingsprincipe is that "de groene hoofdstructuur is leidend" **[D]**. The **Beleidsregel groen en
water bij ruimtelijke ontwikkelingen 2026** (CVDR 766778) is in force from **23 September 2026** and its scope
explicitly includes "aanvragen voor beheer van gemeentelijk groen door derden" and requests to buy or lease municipal
green ground **[D]**. The **Verordening inwonersparticipatie** was amended with effect from **30 June 2026** to add the
**uitdaagrecht** (right to challenge), with an eight-week decision deadline and published reasoning **[D]**. A
**Soortenmanagementplan** line went to the council on 13 May 2025 **[D; content N]**.

**Gatekeeper, documented.** Article 10 of the Beleidsregel is the clearest gatekeeping text found. A request to manage
municipal green of **"groenwaarde basis"** *can* be granted, if the green function is preserved and the arrangement is
secured in a **beheerovereenkomst**. A request for green of **"groenwaarde middel of hoog"** **"wordt afgewezen"** —
rejected — *except* where management passes to "natuur- en terreinbeherende instanties of partijen voor wie de groen-
en natuurwaarde vooropstaat" with demonstrably guaranteed management quality **[D]**. That clause is written for
professional land managers, not a buurttuin. Paired with the uitdaagrecht's refusal grounds — conflicts with adopted
policy, an existing contract with an external party, or the task "niet beter" / dearer **[D]** — a community group has
a formal route to take over a municipal task, and the green rules supply a formal reason to refuse on exactly the
most interesting green. **[I]** This is the sharpest finding here: ambition ("green structure leading", "participate",
"challenge us") and instrument (management only of lowest-value green, only by nature managers) are unreconciled in
any document found.

**Enabler — real but narrowly shaped.** (a) **Groensubsidie**: €5/m² de-paving to a **€2,500** maximum, €25/m² green
roof, €10/m² disconnection — only for the **owner** of a dwelling or premises at least five years old, only physical
measures, before/after photos, a **10-year maintenance obligation**, consent to inspection; tenants excluded, and
collective application only among co-owners via a volmacht **[D]**. An initiative on public or third-party land sits
outside it. (b) Volunteer infrastructure: **'Vrijwillig voor elkaar'** (Dec 2024) carries a **€1,420,000** VSB
volunteer budget and proposes a **flexpool**, an **umbrella foundation** absorbing legal-person burdens and a
**confidential adviser**; it names **regeldruk** as a barrier to reduce **[D]**. (c) Green-compensation money exists
(**€236 per unrealised m²**, 2026) but is developer money, not an initiative budget **[D]**. (d) The
**Wielewachters** "hebben meegedacht" about the estate's walking route, gardens and paths **[D]**. Note the verb:
*meedenken*.

**Absent partner.** (i) **Throughput**: the municipal *Vergroeningsprojecten* dataset (updated 21 Nov 2026) holds
**124 records**: **56** "Kans of wens", **21** "Lopend", **20** "Uitgevoerd", **9** "Afgevoerd", **2** "On hold", 16
unstatused; only **14** have a planned execution year; 90 intake dates run 23 May 2023–15 Sep 2026; **19** rated
"hoog" for resident involvement (33 unrated) **[D, computed from the open export]**. The dataset's own description
says it exists partly to "voorkom[en] het indienen van een wens bij meerdere (mede)financiers… [en] bevordert het
geven van terugkoppeling" **[D]** — the city already knows feedback is the weak link. (ii) **Participation without
influence**: the sibling evidence base's coding of three 2024–2026 files (HOV4, Klimaatadaptatie, Genderwig) found no
resident input changing a project's premise or timing, unaddressed input typically deferred to a document that does
not yet exist, and no published municipal evaluation of participation although the regulation schedules one
**[C — provisional; not accepted findings]**. The `meeweten/meedenken/meewerken/meebeslissen` ladder is defined in the
regulation's own explanatory note, which warns that unclear influence affects "de bereidheid… om aan toekomstige
trajecten deel te nemen" **[D]**. (iii) **No third-party-land instrument**: nothing found addresses a community food
forest on private land — no lease support, no temporary-use agreement, no bridging arrangement — next to €9.8 m for the
municipal estate **[D + I]**.

## 3. Issue #3 — what makes participation last, where care turns into exhaustion

**Barriers, in the city's own words.** 'Vrijwillig voor elkaar' states that "het aantal Eindhovenaren dat zich
vrijwilliger noemt… neemt al jaren af terwijl de vraag naar vrijwilligers toeneemt", and names **regeldruk** —
subsidy procedures, legal-person obligations — as a barrier; it drew on 17 interviews, ~250 survey responses and three
sessions **[D]**. **[I]** A food forest holding a legal entity, insurance, subsidies and event permits is doing
administrative work its volunteers did not sign up for.

**The Dutch green-volunteer picture.** Province Noord-Brabant: roughly **10,000** people active weekly or monthly in
Brabant nature, worth about **€9 m per year** of work; the base is **ageing**; new volunteers want a wide offer, to know
beforehand how much time it takes, flexibility, the ability to **stop easily**, visible impact, and — especially
younger people — to meet people **[D]**. The same piece documents the **"supervrijwilliger"**: a 67-year-old
simultaneously chair, treasurer and secretary of a bird group of nine remaining volunteers averaging 63, saying "ik heb
weleens gevraagd om een opvolger, maar niemand wil" **[D]**. Provincial money flows through organisations (Brabants
Landschap, IVN) and project-shaped instruments (Buurtnatuur- en Buurtwaterfonds, Brabanders voor Natuur) **[D]**.

**Where the local signal fits.** The directory's "running only this year" for *Buurttuin vol Kleur* **[C, provisional
EHV-C-0305]** matches the funder's portfolio: **€5,000** from the Postcode Loterij Fonds, period **"Voorjaar 2026"**,
Groenewoudseweg, Stichting Kansen voor Kinderen with the GroenKLUP **[D]**. A one-season grant produces a one-season
garden — a funding design, not a participation failure **[I]**. The same reading fits the food forest: twelve years of
Fridays, one named front person, a closure notice in the first person singular, a mailbox filling with invitations
elsewhere **[D]** — the *network* outlived the *place*, the reverse of the usual assumption that place comes first.

**International evidence on exhaustion.** The clearest synthesis found (a 2023 Canadian MA thesis — weight it
accordingly) records organiser **burnout and resignation as documented causes of community-garden decline and closure**
(Buenemann 2017; Jain 2012; Ochoa et al. 2019) and notes burnout is *under-studied* in the garden literature though
well documented in volunteerism generally. Its causes — "constant engagement", "endless task switching", "concerns
over funding", "navigating unclear roles or agendas"; organisers **not readily replaceable**, so one burnout loads the
whole core group; **time devoted and extrinsic motives predicting burnout, intrinsic motives and organisational
integration protecting** — map almost one-to-one onto the Brabant and Eindhoven material **[D for the synthesis;
underlying studies second-hand and unverified]**.

**Who carries care; warning signs.** The **initiator/front person** who is also fundraiser and host (all three local
cases have one); the **weekly core** whose rhythm is the institution; the **area coordinator** (39 of 124 greening
wishes entered via "geco", 31 from residents) who converts wishes into municipal records; older volunteers holding
several offices **[D]**. Warning signs the sources support: roles held by one person with no named successor; the same
small group across initiatives; funding shorter than the ecological time of the thing funded; intake without a planned
execution year; input recorded without a published response; closure announced faster than the network can be handed
over — Eindhoven shows the last clearly (planned stop "after the summer", then immediate closure, a workshop still
advertised at the closed site) **[D]**.
