# Voedselbos Eindhoven — a research landscape

A friendly guide to this workspace. It is a landscape of questions, not a work plan.

## What this is

This repository holds candidate research questions about how communities, green spaces and local organisations in Eindhoven can form a mutually supportive ecosystem for human and ecological health through shared care. It grew out of Build for Eindhoven and a conversation with Maartje.

The focus is **Voedselbos Eindhoven and its community** — including the July 2026 closure and the emerging **Voedselbos 2.0**, whose organising focus is **visibility of the movement**. De Wielewaal is context, not the centre.

The main question is in [QUESTION.md](QUESTION.md). The candidate questions are GitHub issues. The first research passes are in [`research/`](research/), and the synthesis is [`research/BRIEF.md`](research/BRIEF.md) — the whole picture in one document.

## How to read the evidence

- **D — documented** in a retrieved source.
- **C — claimed** by one actor or self-description.
- **I — inferred** from structure or context, not stated.
- **U — unexplored** or not established; plausible, no evidence yet.

“Not established” is a finding. No research finding here has been accepted as settled. Dates and access boundaries are in the source lists.

## Working principles

- Research with the community in mind, not on its behalf.
- Do not assume willingness to collaborate.
- Protect sensitive habitats and volunteers' time.
- No stakeholder contact unless explicitly agreed; no implementation.
- If a note contradicts a brief, trust the note and say so.
